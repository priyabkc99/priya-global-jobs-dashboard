MANNARINO Systems & Software Inc. is a leader in providing safety-critical systems, software, hardware and certification engineering services to the aerospace, defense, space, simulation & power generation industries.

Our customer base is very large, covering traditional aerospace companies as well as new and exciting markets, including electric propulsion, Urban Air Mobility, Unmanned Aerial Vehicle and autonomous flight.

The MANNARINO culture is focused on high quality, hard work, professionalism and teamwork within a highly respectful, motivating atmosphere geared towards long-term business growth.

As a member of our team, you will benefit from:


a comprehensive group insurance and RRSP plan
a flextime policy with paid overtime
discretionary bonuses
a fitness reimbursement program
other office perks such as catered weekly lunches and free hot beverages (espresso, coffee, hot chocolate and tea)
regular company outings


Career Opportunity and Primary Roles / Responsibilities


Lead and coordinate all configuration management activities across assigned projects, working closely with Engineering Delivery Managers and key stakeholders.
Adapt and execute the configuration management framework in accordance with contractual, regulatory, and organizational requirements.
Ensure the accuracy, consistency, and completeness of project information within the configuration management system.
Develop, implement, and maintain Configuration Management Plans while considering product line specific requirements.
Manage configuration identification activities, including configuration item selection, product breakdown structures, and baseline requirements.
Organize and facilitate Configuration Control Boards (CCBs).
Establish and maintain configuration baselines, including Functional Baselines (FBL), Allocated Baselines (ABL), and Product Baselines (PBL).
Monitor, record, and report configuration status throughout the project lifecycle.
Plan and coordinate Functional Configuration Audits (FCA) and Physical Configuration Audits (PCA).
Promote configuration management best practices through coaching, guidance, and knowledge sharing with project teams.
Support engineering teams in change management activities and oversee design change implementation.
Ensure effective configuration control throughout system, hardware, and software development and integration activities.


Required Competencies


Bachelor's degree in Engineering or a related technical discipline.
5 to 10 years of experience in aerospace engineering or aerospace product development.
Experience working within the aerospace industry and understanding of regulated development environments.
Strong background in configuration management processes, methodologies, and tools.
Experience with Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) and configuration control practices.
Knowledge of system, hardware, and software integration principles.
Experience establishing and maintaining configuration baselines and managing engineering changes.
Strong organizational, analytical, and problem-solving skills.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management abilities.
Ability to work effectively within multidisciplinary engineering teams.


Miscellaneous Requirements


On-site presence at the Montreal office required a minimum of three (3) days per week; additional on-site attendance may be required during integration phases.


Knowledge of English is required to communicate and work with customers outside the province of Quebec. / La connaissance de l'anglais est requise pour communiquer et travailler avec nos clients situés à l'extérieur de la province de Québec.
