Cognizant is a world leader in delivering digital transformation solutions to Life Sciences. Through our knowledge and expertise in Automation, MES & Digital, we deliver stable manufacturing systems that are optimized to improve operational efficiencies, whilst maintaining compliance.

Our specialist engineering teams have the aptitude to deliver and support Pharma 4.0 architectures and solutions covering all IT, OT and IoT applications, infrastructures and services.

Operating across 5 continents and with over 300.000 skilled employees, Cognizant partners with the leading Life Sciences companies globally to provide performance improvements, reduced costs and improve time to market. We are your single end to end partner for consultancy, design implementation, optimization and support for all applications in the Pharmaceutical and Biotechnology Manufacturing stack.

Cognizant is seeking a DevOps Lead Engineer to join a greenfield MES rollout at a biotech manufacturing site in Limerick, Ireland.

We're looking for someone with a strong track record as a Lead or Senior DevOps Engineer, comfortable pushing configuration changes through dev, test, and live production environments, with solid grounding in version control for MES configuration, CI/CD pipeline design, and automated testing support.

Key Responsibilities


Build, implement, and refine CI/CD pipelines for consistent, dependable deployments across dev, test, and production
Take ownership of the DevOps toolchain and environment lifecycle: source control, infrastructure-as-code, configuration management, release management, monitoring, and observability
Provision and manage secure, resilient, scalable infrastructure underpinning MES platforms, keeping availability and performance high
Manage MES platforms (Emerson Syncade experience preferred) — deployments, configuration, patching, upgrades, and day-to-day operational support
Set and enforce development standards, branching strategy, code review practice, release governance, and change control suited to a regulated manufacturing setting
Work alongside software engineering, QA, MES vendors, system integrators, and business stakeholders to deliver automated deployments and infrastructure-as-code
Build automated testing and quality gates into CI/CD pipelines with QA, pushing shift-left testing and stronger release quality
Stand up monitoring, logging, and alerting to stay ahead of platform health and performance issues
Underpin CSV, audit readiness, disaster recovery, and business continuity with compliant documentation and deployment records
Drive ongoing improvement across the delivery lifecycle, clearing deployment bottlenecks and tracking progress via metrics
Serve as technical authority and escalation point for complex infrastructure, deployment, and operational issues


Requirements


Demonstrated experience as a DevOps Lead or Senior DevOps Engineer on complex enterprise programme
Hands-on development background with a strong grasp of the SDLC and modern DevOps practice
Solid CI/CD, infrastructure-as-code, automation, and scripting skills
Experience managing enterprise application environments — release management, configuration management, infrastructure provisioning
Strong background supporting MES platforms, ideally Emerson Syncade, across deployment, configuration, upgrades, and operational support
Experience in regulated pharma/life sciences environments preferred, with working knowledge of GxP, CSV, and GAMP 5
Familiarity with MES integrations into SAP, LIMS, and historian systems, plus containerisation (Docker, Kubernetes)
Strong problem-solving, communication, and stakeholder management skills
