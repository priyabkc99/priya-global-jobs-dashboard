About Position:




We are seeking a highly skilled Technical Writer to join our Data & AI Engineering team. The ideal candidate will be responsible for creating, maintaining, and managing comprehensive technical documentation for enterprise software, data engineering, cloud, and AI-based solutions. The role requires strong communication skills, attention to detail, and the ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams to translate complex technical concepts into clear, user-friendly documentation.




Role: Technical Writer
Location: Pune
Experience: 5+ Years
Job Type: Full-Time Employment




What You'll Do:




As a Technical Writer, you will be responsible for developing and maintaining technical documentation that supports engineering, data, AI, and cloud-based initiatives across the organization. You will collaborate closely with technical and business stakeholders to ensure the creation of high-quality documentation assets.
Develop and maintain technical documentation including solution designs, architecture documents, user guides, SOPs, runbooks, process flows, and training materials.
Create and manage project artifacts such as requirement specifications, onboarding guides, release notes, governance documents, and process documentation.
Collaborate with engineering, AI, data, cloud, and security teams to gather technical requirements and document system workflows.
Convert complex technical concepts into clear, concise, and audience-appropriate documentation.
Maintain documentation repositories and ensure information is organized, searchable, and up to date.
Ensure documentation accuracy, consistency, version control, and adherence to organizational standards.
Participate in project discussions to capture technical decisions and ensure documentation readiness throughout the project lifecycle.
Support knowledge management initiatives and drive documentation best practices across teams.
Work with stakeholders to review, update, and enhance existing content periodically.
Develop diagrams, workflows, and visual representations of architectures and processes using appropriate documentation tools.




Expertise You'll Bring:




We are looking for a passionate technical documentation professional with strong writing, communication, and stakeholder management skills, capable of delivering high-quality documentation for enterprise technology solutions.
5+ years of experience in technical writing for software, cloud, data engineering, AI/ML, or enterprise platforms.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills with strong attention to detail.
Proven experience creating technical, functional, architectural, and process documentation.
Familiarity with Data Engineering, Cloud, AI/ML, Databricks, and modern enterprise technology ecosystems.
Proficiency with documentation tools such as Confluence, SharePoint, Microsoft Office Suite, Visio, Draw.io, or equivalent tools.
Experience managing document version control and governance practices
Ability to understand technical architectures, workflows, APIs, and system integrations.
Strong organizational and project coordination skills.
Ability to work independently and collaboratively within cross-functional teams.
Experience working in Agile/Scrum environments is preferred.
Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving capabilities.
Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, Technical Communication, or a related discipline.
