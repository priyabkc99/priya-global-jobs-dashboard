Position Overview

Responsible for the management of personnel and administration of the Quality Assurance (QA) Department including the implementation and maintenance of the Quality Assurance Program to assure compliance with contractual requirements, standards and applicable regulations as it applies to IMP Aerospace, Electronic Systems and Canadian Support Helicopters.

What You Will Do


Maintenance of IMP management system documentation
Implementation and monitoring of the internal/external audit program
Corrective action process
Conduct risk, failure and root cause analysis in support of company processes
Non-conforming product and quarantine process
Technical investigations
AVL process including surveys and audits
Quality alerts and bulletins
Material and Warranty Review Boards
Implementation and monitoring of Flight Safety program
Implementation of Quality Final Area Closeout (FACO) activities
Author, assess and approve all levels of management system documentation
Participate and support departmental and company continuous improvement activities
Liaise with Branch/Department Heads and associated managers to support program requirements and participate in the resolution of QMS/technical issues
Be responsible for customer interface requirements on quality issues
Carry out contract reviews identifying Quality & QMS related responsibilities
Maintain the Joint NDQAR Flight Safety Mishap Plan
Assess departmental training requirements and personnel performance
Chair the FOD/Tool Control/Flight Safety Committee
Carry out duties of GIDEP representative
Other as delegated by the Director Quality


What You Will Bring


Engineer, Technologist or Technical Training from the Canadian Forces or a Transport Canada approved training organization or equivalent background of training and experience in the aircraft maintenance/manufacturing industry
Trained in the field of Quality Assurance/Quality Management & Auditing
Minimum 10 years aviation industry experience
Minimum 5 years in a Quality Assurance role
Minimum of 5 years in a management capacity with emphasis on Quality
Experience and knowledge with DND Flight Safety System or SMS is an asset
Must be eligible to meet requirements for Canadian Controlled Goods Program (CGP), U.S. International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR) and Canadian government security clearances
