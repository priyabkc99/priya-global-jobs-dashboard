Technical Writer -ScaleFlux Bangalore, Karnataka, India




At ScaleFlux, we are a family unit powered by diversity, inclusion, transparency, respect, integrity, and passion—for both our clients and our people. Our business growth depends on your professional development, as we collaborate, share ideas and innovations, and invest in our future. By forging meaningful partnerships with our people, we come together with a shared purpose, exceeding our goals. This keeps us nimble, ahead of the competition, and at the forefront of our industry. Our continued success begins with you.




ScaleFlux helps customers harness data growth as a competitive advantage by building products that reduce complexity and accelerate the creation of value from data. In our first phase of rethinking the data pipeline for the modern data center, ScaleFlux built a better SSD by embedding computational storage technology into flash drives. Now, customers can gain an edge by deploying storage intelligence to optimize their data center infrastructure for workloads like databases, analytics, IoT, and 5G.




For detailed information about us visit the company website:www.scaleflux.com




Responsibilities:

Create complex product documentation, including technical specifications, datasheets, programming guide, and register documents
Gather and synthesize information from a variety of internal sources and documents within the organization
Collaborate with engineering to obtain content and ensure accuracy
Translate complex concepts into easy-to-understand content
Create diagrams and tables to clarify complex ideas
Ability to interpret technical drawings, and design documentation
Develop document automation tools to improve efficiency




Required

3+ years writing for semiconductor or embedded system domains
Ability and desire to learn new tools and technologies
Results-driven self-starter, able to work independently to achieve success
Strong time-management skills in a fast-paced and demanding environment
Fluent in English, with excellent oral and written communication skills
Experience with tools such as FrameMaker, Atlassian tools, Draw.io (Jira, Confluence, etc.)
Strong analytical and research abilities and manage multiple documentation projects




Preferred

Bachelor of Science degree in a science or engineering related field with formal training in Technical Writing, or Bachelor of English, Journalism with experience in hardware industry
Experience with Mark-down, DITA, XML-structured authoring, single-sourcing principles
