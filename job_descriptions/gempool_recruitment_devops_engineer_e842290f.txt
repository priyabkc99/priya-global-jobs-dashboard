DevOps Engineer

Location: Dublin / Belfast / Malta

Salary: Up to €90,000 / £78,000

Hybrid: 2–3 days onsite

Full-Time | Permanent




Gempool is partnering with a leading international technology-driven organisation to hire an experienced DevOps Engineer to join a growing engineering team across Dublin, Belfast, or Malta.




This is an exciting opportunity for a Platform/DevOps Engineer with strong Kubernetes and AWS experience to help drive cloud modernisation, platform engineering initiatives, and infrastructure scalability within a highly secure and regulated environment.




The Role

You will work closely with engineering and infrastructure teams to design, implement, and optimise cloud-native platforms while improving automation, observability, and deployment processes.




Responsibilities

Design, implement, and maintain Kubernetes (EKS) platforms on AWS
Support and optimise CI/CD pipelines using GitHub Actions and Harness.io
Implement and manage observability tooling across the platform using Datadog
Enhance and support AWS cloud infrastructure environments
Review, optimise, and document deployment and operational processes
Ensure change management processes align with security and compliance standards
Collaborate with development teams to enable self-service platform capabilities
Support incident response and platform reliability initiatives
Analyse and optimise cloud infrastructure costs




Required Skills & Experience

3+ years’ experience in a senior DevOps or Platform Engineering role
Strong hands-on experience with Kubernetes, particularly AWS EKS
Experience working with AWS ECS environments
Strong knowledge of AWS services across compute, databases, and security
Experience with Infrastructure as Code tools such as Terraform
Hands-on experience with observability and monitoring tools, ideally Datadog
Strong scripting skills using Python and/or Bash
Good understanding of container security best practices
Experience working within regulated or security-focused environments
Strong communication skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines




Nice to Have

Experience with GitOps workflows and tooling
Exposure to service mesh technologies such as Istio
Understanding of platform engineering concepts
AWS cost optimisation experience
Linux or database administration experience
Experience working within financial services environments
Agile or SAFe methodology experience
