RESPONSIBILITIES (Key Accountabilities And Responsibilities)

The Configuration Manager is responsible for:


To provide functional leadership of the configuration management discipline at the domain level.
The definition during the bid phase of the scope of work for configuration management activities during the program.
The (where delegated by the Project Manager) execution of the work package manager role for the configuration management work packages on programs.
The analysis of contract requirements with respect to configuration management during the bid phase.
The interaction with the customer to clarify or adjust configuration management requirements.
The management of defined baselines and customer configuration management related requirements for a program/product line.
The creation and maintenance of the Configuration Management Plan (CMP) for the program/product lines.
Working with Customers, Partners, Sub-contractors and Suppliers to:
assess their configuration management capability;
to manage the flow-down of configuration management requirements via a defined statement of work (SOW);
audit the implementation of configuration management requirements by sub-contractors and suppliers.
To actively manage the configuration management interactions across the Thales to Customer/Partner/Subcontractor/Supplier boundaries to ensure the efficient & effective management of build to spec, or design to spec practices.
Working with engineering staff to determine the choice of, using the defined criteria:
of configuration items;
of items to be controlled.
The organisation of the product structure of common elements, variants and options, in support of, and with the agreement of the Program/Product Line Manager.
The definition of, and organising and steering the Configuration Control Board (CCB) including pre and post CCB activities (including clerical work).
The management of Contract, Functional, Allocated and Product Configuration Baselines (CBL, FBL, ABL, PBL) for the program/product line.
The provision to the customer, under project management control, of configuration management deliverables.
The verification and audit of the contents and integrity of the CMS.
The provision of configuration management support of engineering and program phase reviews and milestone events.
Ensuring that the program/product lines are fully and comprehensively managed within the CMS.
The conduct of Functional and Physical Configuration Audits. (FCA, PCA) for the program/product line.
The coordination of the configuration management actions across departments for program/product lines.
Working with program/product line management, and sales & marketing to cater for the creation of new versions of the product or system.
The line management of the staff in the configuration management team.
other related duties as assigned.


Qualifications

KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS & EXPERIENCE


Graduate Technical or Business qualification(s) or equivalent in a related area of Configuration Management.



Essential Experience


For a graduate experience in a related technical discipline or at least extensive experience in Configuration Management.
Knowledge of Product Lifecycle Management.
Demonstrated communication skills.
Solid experience with Tools such as PTC Windchill or Siemens Teamcenter or equivalent
Knowledge of configuration Management standards & processes.
Demonstrated leadership, negotiation and coordination skills.
Knowledge of the Systems Engineering discipline



Desirable Experience


Systems Engineering, Software Engineering, Hardware Engineering, Customer Service Engineering.
Relevant experience in standards such as SAE EIA-649, MIL-HDBK-61B, ISO 10007, etc
Team management.
Transversal management.
Knowledge of the product.
