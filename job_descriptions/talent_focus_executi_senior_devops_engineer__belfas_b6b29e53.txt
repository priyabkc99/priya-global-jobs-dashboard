Senior DevOps Engineer – Belfast

Hybrid – minimum 2 days per week in the Belfast office | Full Time | Permanent




We’re partnering with a growing international technology business to appoint a Senior DevOps Engineer to join its Belfast-based Engineering team.




This is a hands-on role for an experienced DevOps / SRE / Platform Engineer who enjoys building reliable infrastructure, automating deployments and improving the way software moves from development into production.




You’ll play a key role in managing infrastructure, developing CI/CD pipelines, increasing automation and helping engineering teams deliver software faster and more reliably.




What you’ll be doing

Build, manage and improve CI/CD pipelines
Automate infrastructure and deployment processes
Work extensively with Kubernetes, Terraform and Jenkins
Manage Linux-based infrastructure
Support private and public cloud environments at scale
Improve reliability, monitoring and platform performance
Collaborate closely with software engineering and operations teams
Help establish scalable infrastructure standards and best practices




What we’re looking for

You’ll ideally have 4+ years’ experience within DevOps, SRE or Platform Engineering, together with strong hands-on experience across:

Kubernetes
Terraform
Jenkins
Linux
Public and private cloud infrastructure
GitHub / Bitbucket or similar version control
Scripting with Python, Bash, Groovy, Java and/or Perl




Experience with Docker, Grafana, Prometheus, Datadog, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, enterprise-scale automation or cybersecurity would be advantageous.




The opportunity

You’ll join an international technology business developing software focused on data understanding, classification, security and risk management, working within an environment where cloud infrastructure, automation and platform reliability are central to the product.

The package includes pension, health and wellness benefits, generous holiday allowance, flexible working and hybrid working.
