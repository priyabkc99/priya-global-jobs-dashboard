As a DevOps Engineer you will be part of the Software Tooling Team, focusing on optimizing and enhancing our internal software development lifecycle. Key responsibilities include enhancing the software build and deployment processes for our C++/Python SaaS medical device; overseeing the deployment and maintenance of internal services, including Artifactory and Vault; developing and maintaining internal Jenkins pipelines; and writing tools in Golang and Python to improve automation and support the software development teams.

The Role will include

·        Deploying and maintaining internal software services

·        Creating and supporting internal build pipelines and tools

·        Assisting development teams with bug fixes and feature requests

Experience Required

·        Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related fields.

·        5 years of professional experience in Software Development or DevOps roles

·        Strong experience in relevant languages, such as Golang or Python

·        A solid understanding of the Software Development Lifecyle and how tooling and platform engineering can help accelerate development

·        Experience in managing cloud infrastructure using Terraform and Helm

Familiarity with Continuous Integration (CI) practices and tools, such as Jenkins or similar platforms

    What we can offer you:

·        Hybrid working - 2 days from home

·        Easy access to Public Transport and very close to the University of Melbourne, Lygon St and the CBD

·        New, comfortable office space with end of trip facilities

·        Bonus annual leave scheme as well as company paid parental leave

·        Be a part of our wellness program including free fruit, coffee, table tennis, free gym and corporate class pass, and an Employee Assistance Program for you and your family

·        Rewards and Recognition Program including discounts for shopping and entertainment

·        Laptop and accessories provided for remote work

·        Regular social events
