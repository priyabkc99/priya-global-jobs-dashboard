DevOps Engineer St Julian's, Malta to €60,000




Are you a technologist focused DevOps Engineer looking for an opportunity to progress your career?




You could be joining a growing technology company with a successful taxi booking platform and B2B customer analytics suite.




As a DevOps Engineer you will join a small team to help build, automate and optimise the platforms that enable the engineering teams to deliver high quality software at scale. Collaborating with Development and Infrastructure teams, you'll play a key role in improving developer productivity, streamlining CI/CD pipelines and evolving the cloud infrastructure that supports the products.




This is a hands-on role where you'll automate build, test and release processes, manage containerised environments and help shape the roadmap for infrastructure and platform engineering. You'll collaborate with software engineers to design reliable deployment pipelines, provision new environments and continuously improve the speed, security and reliability of software delivery.




Location / WFH:

You'll join the team in St. Julian's three days, with flexibility to work from home twice a week.




About you:

You have strong DevOps experience with a technology product based company
You have a good knowledge of Docker, Kubernetes and Terraform and are proficient with development of CI/CD pipelines
You have GCP or AWS cloud infrastructure experience
You're collaborative with fluent English language skills




What's in it for you:

Salary to €60,000
Hybrid working
Range of benefits
