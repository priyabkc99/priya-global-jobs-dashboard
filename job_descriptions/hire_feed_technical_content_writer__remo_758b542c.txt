Role: Technical Content Writer (Remote)
Location: Remote (Work from Anywhere)




Role Overview:

We are hiring for one of our clients, seeking a Technical Content Writer to work on a Full-Time basis. The role involves producing clear, technically accurate content and supporting data-driven work with hands-on experience using JSON and public datasets. You will collaborate with researchers and stakeholders to translate complex findings into concise narratives and actionable recommendations.




Key Responsibilities:

• Produce clear, well-structured technical documentation and written deliverables.

• Analyze public datasets to derive business insights and respond to key analytical questions.

• Clearly explain reasoning and logic in notebooks or other suitable formats.

• Work with JSON-based data and ensure outputs are accurate and well-organized.

• Use Python scripting (as needed) to support data exploration, validation, and reproducible analysis.




Required Skills & Qualifications:

• Minimum 3+ years of relevant experience as a technical content writer, analyst, or software developer.

• Strong technical writing skills with experience producing clear, accurate, and structured content.

• Comfort working with data formats like JSON and interpreting datasets.

• Working knowledge of Python for data exploration/validation (not a core engineering role).

• Familiarity with notebooks and/or scripting workflows to support analysis and documentation.




More About the Opportunity:

This role offers a unique opportunity to work with a global leader in the Technology, Information and Internet industry, contributing to the transformation of AI from proof of concept into proprietary intelligence. The position emphasizes collaboration with top AI researchers and delivery of measurable impact.




Equal Opportunity Employer:

We hire based on skills and expertise. All qualified candidates are welcome regardless of background, experience, or prior employment history. Applications are reviewed solely on demonstrated technical ability and qualifications.
