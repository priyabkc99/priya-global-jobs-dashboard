🚀 2 x Release Managers – SaaS | Fully Remote

💰 £55,000–£65,000 + Bonus + Excellent Benefits

🏡 Fully Remote + occasional fully expensed travel

📈 Series B | High Growth | SaaS | Tech for Good

Code ready doesn't mean launch ready.

We're partnering with a high-growth Series B SaaS business looking for two experienced Release Managers to own end-to-end release readiness across Product, Engineering and GTM.

Your job is ultimately to answer one question:

Are we genuinely ready to launch?

That means making sure Engineering is ready, Product is aligned, dependencies are resolved, testing is complete, Customer Success and Support know what's coming, demos and documentation are prepared, and the wider GTM organisation is ready before anything reaches customers.

SaaS experience is essential.

🚀 What you'll own

• End-to-end SaaS releases from planning through to launch

• Release plans, readiness criteria and quality gates

• Dependencies, risks, blockers and clear ownership

• Alignment across Product, Engineering, Customer Success, Support, Marketing & GTM

• Customer testing, demos, early access and phased rollouts

• Release communications, documentation and stakeholder updates

• Post-release reviews and continuous improvement

• Improving the processes, tooling and automation behind releases as the business scales

But this role is about more than process.

You need presence.

You'll bring teams together, ask difficult questions, challenge when something isn't ready and hold people accountable for what they've committed to.

You're the person asking:

What's outstanding? Who owns it? What's blocking us? Are our teams ready? Can we launch?

🌟 What we're looking for

✓ Strong SaaS experience – essential

✓ Proven end-to-end software/product release experience

✓ Experience working across Product + Engineering + GTM

✓ Strong Agile / SDLC knowledge

✓ Excellent stakeholder management and communication

✓ Strong risk, dependency and blocker management

✓ Experience improving release processes in a scaling environment

✓ Confidence to challenge, influence and make decisions

You might currently be a:

Release Manager | Software Release Manager | Product Operations Manager | Technical Programme Manager

🌱 Why join?

This isn't about inheriting a finished release process and maintaining it.

It's an opportunity to help build what great looks like.

You'll have genuine influence over how a rapidly scaling SaaS organisation gets products and features successfully out of the door — creating the structure, visibility and accountability needed across Product, Engineering and GTM.

If you know how to get SaaS releases out successfully — and how to get the whole business ready for them — I want to hear from you.
