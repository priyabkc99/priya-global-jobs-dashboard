The Release Manager / Environment Manager will be responsible for owning and managing the release processes across DEV/SIT, UAT, Preprod, and Production environments. This role ensures that all environments remain in sync, coordinates cutovers with infrastructure and DevOps teams, and drives smooth, controlled deployments across the delivery lifecycle.

Key Responsibilities:



Manage and oversee end-to-end release processes across all environments.

Ensure environment consistency, version control, and alignment between development and production.

Coordinate release cutovers and deployments with cross-functional teams (infra, DevOps, application owners).

Track and report release progress and issues using established tools and governance frameworks.

Work closely with project and operations teams to ensure release readiness and minimize downtime.



Required Skills:



Strong experience in Agile/Scrum environments.

Proficiency in release orchestration and environment management.

Hands-on experience with Jira and ServiceNow.

Excellent communication and cross-team coordination skills.
