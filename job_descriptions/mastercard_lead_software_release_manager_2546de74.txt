Title And Summary

Lead Software Release Manager

Overview

We are seeking an experienced Software Release Manager to lead end-to-end release planning, scheduling, and orchestration across a portfolio of 7-8 interconnected software products and platforms. This role is responsible for establishing and maintaining an integrated release calendar that balances product roadmaps, engineering capacity, testing environment availability, customer commitments, regulatory requirements, and cross-program dependencies.

The ideal candidate thrives in complex enterprise environments where multiple teams, systems, and stakeholders must coordinate to successfully deliver high-quality software releases. This individual will serve as the central point of coordination for release readiness, dependency management, environment planning, and deployment execution.

Key Responsibilities

Release Planning & Scheduling


Own the enterprise release calendar across 7-8 products and platforms.
Develop and maintain integrated release schedules accounting for:
Development milestones
Integration testing windows
Pre-production validation
Performance and scalability testing
Production deployment windows
Customer onboarding and certification timelines
Coordinate release trains and major program increments across multiple teams.
Balance competing priorities while optimizing release frequency and stability.



Dependency Management


Identify, document, track, and proactively manage:
Product-to-product dependencies
Program-to-program dependencies
Infrastructure dependencies
Vendor and external partner dependencies
Customer readiness dependencies
Facilitate dependency review forums and drive cross-functional alignment.
Resolve schedule conflicts and escalation issues impacting release delivery.



Environment & Capacity Management


Govern utilization of critical testing and deployment environments, including:
Integration environments
System testing environments
UAT environments
Performance testing environments
Pre-production environments
Production environments
Coordinate environment reservations and conflict resolution across multiple teams.
Ensure environment readiness, availability, and release sequencing.



Release Readiness & Governance


Establish release entry and exit criteria.
Conduct release readiness reviews and go/no-go assessments.
Verify completion of:
Functional testing
Integration testing
Regression testing
Performance testing
Security validation
Operational readiness activities
Ensure change management and release governance processes are followed.



Stakeholder Coordination


Partner closely with:
Engineering teams
Product Managers
Program Managers
Quality Engineering teams
Site Reliability Engineering teams
Infrastructure teams
Customer delivery teams
Operations and Support organizations
Communicate release plans, risks, constraints, and status to both technical and executive audiences.
Drive alignment across globally distributed teams.



Risk & Issue Management


Identify schedule, environment, resource, and operational risks early.
Develop mitigation and contingency plans.
Lead release war rooms for major deployments.
Track and drive resolution of release blockers and critical dependencies.



Metrics & Continuous Improvement


Define and monitor release management KPIs, including:
Release predictability
On-time delivery
Deployment success rate
Environment utilization
Defect leakage
Change failure rate
Continuously improve release planning processes, tooling, and governance.



All About You


Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Information Technology, Engineering, or related discipline.
8+ years of experience in software delivery, technical program management, release management, or engineering operations.
5+ years managing enterprise-scale software releases.
Experience coordinating releases across multiple products and engineering teams simultaneously.
Strong understanding of:
SDLC
Agile and Scrum methodologies
DevOps practices
CI/CD pipelines
Change and release management processes
Demonstrated experience managing complex dependency chains and cross-functional delivery programs.
Strong project planning, organization, and risk-management skills.
Excellent executive communication and stakeholder-management capabilities.



Preferred Qualifications


Experience in large-scale distributed systems or payment-processing platforms.
Experience coordinating releases across globally distributed teams.
Working knowledge of tools such as:
Jira
Azure DevOps
GitHub Actions
Jenkins
XL Release / Digital.ai Release
Splunk
Confluence
PMP, PMI-ACP, SAFe, ITIL, or DevOps certifications.
Experience supporting highly regulated or mission-critical production environments.



Success Measures

A Successful Software Release Manager Will


Maintain a synchronized release schedule across 7-8 products with minimal conflicts.
Improve release predictability and on-time delivery.
Reduce environment contention and resource bottlenecks.
Ensure all customer, platform, program, and infrastructure dependencies are proactively managed.
Deliver production releases with high deployment success rates and low operational impact.
Provide leadership visibility into release health, risks, and readiness.
