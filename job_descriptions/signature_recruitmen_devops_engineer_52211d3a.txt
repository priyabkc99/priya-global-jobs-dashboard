DevOps Engineer | Bristol | £50,000 - £60,000 | Permanent

About the Role

Signature Recruitment is delighted to be supporting an innovative, high-growth organisation in the search for a DevOps Engineer. This is a unique opportunity to take ownership of critical infrastructure and data systems that support cutting-edge operations and business growth.

As a DevOps Engineer, you will inherit a modern technical environment and play a key role in maintaining, improving and evolving core systems. You'll work closely with technical specialists and operational teams to ensure reliability, scalability and performance.

Key Responsibilities

Own, operate and improve the organisation's time-series data platform
Maintain and extend infrastructure using Infrastructure as Code, Docker and GitOps approaches
Manage edge devices and on-site collection systems
Build and maintain monitoring and observability tools
Support integration between hardware, control systems and software platforms
Create and maintain technical documentation and operational runbooks

What We're Looking For

Strong experience in Systems, Infrastructure, Platform or DevOps Engineering
Strong Linux administration and networking skills
Experience with Docker, Terraform/OpenTofu and CI/CD pipelines
Exposure to cloud platforms and on-premise infrastructure
Understanding of SQL databases, MQTT or similar messaging technologies
A pragmatic, curious mindset with a passion for continuous improvement
Comfortable working across teams and taking ownership of critical systems

Location & Working Hours

Bristol
Flexible hybrid working with an expectation of 2-3 days onsite
Monday to Friday, 9am-5pm
Parking available

Why Join?

Genuine ownership of business-critical systems
Varied work across cloud, infrastructure, data and hardware environments
Opportunity to shape technical direction and future architecture
Join a mission-driven, innovative organisation with ambitious growth plans
Supportive team environment with freedom to learn and develop

Call to Action

If you're an experienced DevOps Engineer looking for a role that offers real ownership, impact and variety, we'd love to hear from you. Contact Julianna Suki at Signature Recruitment today to discuss this DevOps Engineer opportunity and take the next step in your career.
