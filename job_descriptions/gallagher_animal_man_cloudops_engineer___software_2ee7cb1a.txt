Build the Cloud Platform Behind Global AgTech Innovation

At eShepherd, we're transforming livestock farming through world-leading virtual fencing technology.

Our platform combines solar-powered neckbands, mobile applications and cloud software to help farmers manage livestock without physical fences. With deployments across Australia, New Zealand, North America and beyond, we're scaling rapidly and looking for a CloudOps Engineer to help build the platform that powers it all.

This is an opportunity to work on a global IoT platform, solve complex engineering challenges and have a direct impact on technology used every day by farmers around the world.

The Opportunity

As our customer base, device volumes and product offerings grow, so does the complexity of the cloud infrastructure supporting them.

You'll help ensure our platform remains secure, reliable, scalable and ready for growth by:


Designing, building and optimising AWS cloud infrastructure
Improving platform reliability, performance and scalability
Building and maintaining Infrastructure as Code, automation and CI/CD pipelines
Strengthening monitoring, observability and incident response practices
Supporting multi-region deployments and global platform architecture
Improving database performance and operational efficiency
Investigating and solving complex performance challenges before customers experience them
Working closely with software engineers to deliver secure, scalable solutions


This is a hands-on role with plenty of autonomy, ownership and influence.

What You'll Bring

You'll enjoy solving difficult technical problems and understanding how systems work beneath the surface.

Ideally, You'll Bring


Experience operating and scaling production AWS environments
Strong DevOps and cloud engineering capability
Experience with ECS, EKS or containerised platforms
Infrastructure as Code experience, ideally Terraform
Strong CI/CD and automation skills
Experience with monitoring, alerting and incident response
Database optimisation and performance tuning experience
Python, Node.js or similar scripting capability
Strong communication skills and the ability to collaborate across distributed teams


Highly Regarded


IoT platforms and connected devices
MQTT, LTE-M, NB-IoT or LoRa
Grafana, Prometheus or similar tooling
Multi-region cloud deployments
DevSecOps practices
High-volume telemetry or time-series workloads


Why This Role Is Different

The systems you build don't just support software. They help farmers manage livestock, improve productivity and enhance animal welfare.

Your Work Will Influence


A rapidly growing global technology platform
Thousands of connected devices in the field
Customer experience and platform reliability
The future of digital agriculture


You'll see the real-world impact of what you build.

Supporting a Global Customer Base

A key aspect of this role is supporting our growing North American customer base.

To provide strong coverage during US business hours, this role requires flexibility to work outside standard New Zealand business hours when needed. We recognise this commitment and offer a compensation package that reflects the additional flexibility and coverage responsibilities associated with the role.

Benefits We Offer

We know talented engineers have options, so we invest in creating an environment where great people can do meaningful work and continue to grow their careers.

We Offer


Competitive salary + performance incentives
Real career growth as we scale products globally
Modern tools, technology and equipment to do your best work
A practical, collaborative team that gets things done
KiwiSaver contributions
Boost App membership and staff discounts across New Zealand
Life & Disability Insurance for added peace of mind
Free parking, subsidised on-site café and excellent amenities on our Hamilton campus
Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)
Ongoing learning and development opportunities
Flexible working arrangements
The backing of Gallagher's global network combined with the pace and innovation of a high-growth technology business


If you're excited by cloud infrastructure, AWS, platform engineering and building technology that is transforming agriculture globally, we'd love to hear from you.

Applications will be reviewed as they are received and an appointment may be made before the closing date, so don't delay in applying.
