The candidate will support the Halifax Class Combat Systems Integration (CSI) In Service Support (ISS) program and will be called to work at Canadian Forces Base (CFB) Stadacona in Halifax once the necessary security clearances have been granted by the Government of Canada (CANADA). They will function within a multi-disciplined team environment and perform engineering tasks spanning the complete life cycle of the systems from specification, design, development, integration & test, deployment, maintenance and field troubleshooting.




The candidate will participate in engineering activities including but not limited to:

Act as the day to day steward of the IBM Rational Dynamic Object-Oriented Requirements System (DOORS) repository: create and modify requirement modules, apply attribute sets, run query based reports, and enforce version control best practices
Capture the linkages between requirements, design, and test artifacts—maintaining full traceability from inception to product release
Work with test, engineering, software and quality teams to refine DOORS workflows and templates, ensuring alignment with global release & compliance objectives
Test Witnessing
Test Plan/Test Sheet reviews
Task /Preliminary/Detailed/Critical Design Reviews
Walkthrough Design Reviews
Supporting Test/Trials
Performing requirements analysis
Performing trade studies
System architecture and design
System integration planning
Design documentation
Following systems engineering processes and best practices.




Required Skills and Experience:

Possess bachelor’s degree from an accredited college in a related discipline, or equivalent experience/combined education, with five years of professional experience
Experience in requirements definition/development/maintenance
Knowledge of IBM Rational DOORS
Relationship building skills
Excellent written and verbal communication; proven ability to translate complex technical requirements into clear, actionable documentation
Strong grasp of requirements engineering fundamentals (traceability, impact analysis, change control)
Organizational and planning skills
Works well individually or in a team environment
Ability to acquire North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) Secret Security clearance.




Desired Skills and Experience:

Knowledge of Redmine and Windchill
Knowledge of Requirements Management System (RMS)
Knowledge of Test Management System (TMS)
Knowledge of Technical Statement of Requirements (TSORs)
Knowledge of the Halifax Class Combat System Integration Performance Specification (CSIPS)
Experience with the Combat Management System 330 (CMS 330)
Experience with Agile methodology
Experience in the Navy or Defence domains
Experience with Change Management, Test and Trials (T&T), Security, Safety, Electromagnetic Interference (EMI)/Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC) or Environmental Qualification.
