Change, Release and Configuration Manager

Contract through to 31 December 2026 (with possibility of extension)
Osborne Park
37.5 hours per week
$65 p/h + Super




Your new company

Join a public sector organisation undergoing continued improvement across its technology services environment. The organisation is seeking an experienced Change, Release and Configuration Manager to provide leadership, governance, and professional guidance across its IT service management processes.




Your new role

Reporting within Technology Services, you will provide direction and leadership across Change, Configuration and Release Management. You will support operational and project teams in understanding and appropriately incorporating these disciplines into their activities, while providing professional advice to management, business stakeholders, system owners, and Information and Technology staff.




Key responsibilities will include:

Coordinating the Change Management function and ensuring Requests for Change are appropriately categorised, assessed, prioritised, compliant, and approved before implementation
Investigating unauthorised, failed, and emergency changes to identify and action improvement opportunities
Overseeing Configuration Management, with a particular focus on Software Asset Management, asset management, and service and infrastructure configuration
Managing the release lifecycle, including scheduling, cross-team coordination, and deployment
Ensuring testing, validation, and configuration management are incorporated into release and deployment activities
Establishing, maintaining, and continually improving operational processes, templates, and governance artefacts
Monitoring process compliance and preparing management reports
Chairing the Change Advisory Board and managing associated artefacts, including Terms of Reference and minutes
Collaborating with other ITIL process owners to improve service delivery and promote active ITIL processes
Managing Asset Team employees and contractors, including interviewing, performance management, skills assessment, training, and mentoring




What you'll need to succeed

To succeed in this role, you will bring demonstrated experience across Change, Release and Configuration Management. You will be confident applying governance and best-practice processes, advising senior and technical stakeholders, managing compliance, and coordinating releases across multiple teams. Experience with Software Asset Management, ITIL processes, Change Advisory Boards, and team leadership will be central to the position.




You will also demonstrate strong leadership behaviours and contribute positively to a values-led organisational culture.




What you'll get in return

This is an opportunity to take ownership of critical IT service management functions within a complex public sector technology environment.
