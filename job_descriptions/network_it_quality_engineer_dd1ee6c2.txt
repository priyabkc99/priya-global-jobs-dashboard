Passionate about software quality and automation? A growing technology business is looking for a Quality Engineer to help drive testing excellence across development teams. If you have experience with automated testing, enjoy identifying defects before they reach production, and want to play a key role in improving software quality, this hybrid opportunity offers the chance to work with modern testing tools and influence QA best practices across the business.
Duties & Responsibilities
Qualifications:

Relevant IT qualification advantageous
Software Testing or QA certifications beneficial
Requirements:

Experience in Software Testing, QA, or Quality Engineering
Experience with Playwright or similar automation testing tools
Manual and automated testing experience
Azure DevOps experience
Experience with regression, smoke, and functional testing
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
SQL knowledge advantageous
KPAs:

Create and maintain automated test suites using Playwright
Perform manual and exploratory testing where required
Investigate and recreate software defects
Support release readiness and quality assurance processes
Improve testing standards, documentation, and QA practices
Collaborate with developers, Product Owners, and stakeholders to ensure quality delivery
