Murrin Murrin Operations has an opportunity for an experienced Technical Writer to join our established and dedicated Human Resources training team based on site at Murrin Murrin Operations.

Murrin Murrin Operations is Australia’s largest cobalt producer and a major world producer of nickel. Our Murrin Murrin site in WA’s northern Goldfields is an integrated nickel and cobalt mining and processing operation employing over 1,000 people. We are a diverse, dedicated and driven workforce, building a sustainable business today with a focus on tomorrow.

Murrin Murrin Operations is wholly owned by Glencore, one of the world’s largest globally diversified natural resources companies and a major producer and marketer of more than 60 commodities. Glencore employs over 145,000 people operates around 150 operating mining and processing sites, oil production assets and agricultural facilities in over 35 countries.

The Role

The successful person will become a member of a hardworking and committed team who are responsible for the reviewing and refining existing documentation, identifying areas requiring clarification and addressing needs as they arise, and gathering information from trainers, Subject Matter Experts (SMEs), and internal stakeholders.

The Technical Writer develops high-quality training materials, technical documents, work instructions, and user guides to enhance workforce capability and knowledge.

You will be responsible for:


Developing content to be utilised across multiple channels.
Interpreting and clarifying technical content information copy to deliver engaging, accurate and compliant technical content/documentation.
Maintaining a high level of health, safety and environmental awareness and compliance with all relevant policies and procedures.
Maintaining a high level of health, safety and environmental awareness and compliance with all relevant policies and procedures.
Maintaining accurate records and version controls of all documentation
Maintaining currency in all training: work licences and other subject matter


About You

This role would suit a technically competent and driven professional communicator skilled in training and knowledge transfer ideally looking for a new challenge in our multifaceted processing plant.

You will also have:


Advanced diploma in a technical or scientific field with at least 3 years of professional experience in a design/methods office or Graduate diploma/master’s degree in a technical or scientific field
Comprehensive knowledge of vocational training legislation
Strong project management capabilities
Proficient in computer skills, including the use of integrated management systems, word processing, presentation, and spreadsheet applications
Ability to deliver training and effectively engage groups in learning
A current & valid C class manual driver’s licence


What We Offer

We offer a diverse range of roles across our multi-pit mining operations through to our complex and technically challenging hydrometallurgical process plant.

Also on offer are:


A permanent position working directly for Murrin Murrin Operations
The opportunity to work for a progressive industry leader in nickel mining and processing
A work environment that prides itself on high safety standards
A range of health and lifestyle benefits including our Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
A variable Compensation Program that creates a link between individual performance and company success
The opportunity to take advantage of our impressive 100 kilometres of cycling and running tracks, premium on-site gym facilities with weekly fitness and sport programs
An above industry standard superannuation contribution of 15%
A generous private health insurance allowance
Well established on-the-job training opportunities and development programs to further your technical and leadership skills
The ability to salary sacrifice flights, parking, super and novated leasing etc.
