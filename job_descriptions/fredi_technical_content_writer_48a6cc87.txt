FREDi is on the lookout for a Technical Content Writer who can turn complex logistics tech into clear, impactful, and user-friendly content. If you have a knack for explaining APIs, dashboards, and software workflows in a way that both developers and customers understand — we’d love to talk to you.

What You’ll Do


Create and maintain product documentation, API guides, and user manuals
Write technical blogs, feature updates, FAQs, and onboarding flows
Collaborate with product and engineering teams to translate features into documentation
Break down logistics and shipping concepts into readable, helpful content
Support marketing with whitepapers, case studies, and explainers
4–6 years of experience in technical writing (B2B SaaS/logistics a plus)
Strong grasp of software, APIs, and product workflows
Excellent English writing skills and attention to detail
Familiarity with tools like Notion, Markdown, Confluence, or Git
Bonus: Experience writing for developer or enterprise tech audiences


Why Join FREDi?


FREDi is India’s modern logistics management platform — built to simplify shipping, tracking, returns, and carrier management for high-volume businesses. We're backed by BEO Software, Germany, bringing years of global expertise to power local innovation.
At FREDi, you’ll work in a product-led, fast-moving environment with the stability of global expertise behind you. We value curiosity, ownership, and collaboration — and we’re proud to be a great place to work.
