As a Senior DevOps Engineer, you will bring a cross-disciplinary skill set that spans the cloud, development, operations, continuous delivery, data, and security.




For this role, you’ll need a deep understanding and experience in continuous integration and continuous delivery (CICCD) theory, concepts and real-world application. You need to be experienced with CI/CD tools and systems, and understand their setup, so you can integrate them to create fully functioning, cohesive delivery pipelines and commit, merge, build, test, package, and deploy code.

Work within agile software delivery teams on customer sites
Help our clients maintain and run reliable applications and systems
Promote sound software engineering and DevOps practices
Apply best practices to security in everything you do
Keep your skills up to date and always be looking for continuous improvement
Exploration and innovation - understand client workflows, including strengths and weaknesses, to identify new tech/solutions/improvements to make processes more efficient
Develop documentation as good as the solutions you create
Use automation and testing to make systems not only fast butalso reliable and repeatable
