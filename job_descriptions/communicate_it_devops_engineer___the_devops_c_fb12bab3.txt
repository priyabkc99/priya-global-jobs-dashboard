Every great performance needs a conductor.
Behind every seamless deployment, scalable platform, and reliable system is a DevOps Engineer ensuring every moving part works together in harmony. From cloud infrastructure and automation to CI/CD pipelines and monitoring, DevOps professionals orchestrate the technology that keeps modern businesses running at their best.
We're building our network of talented DevOps Engineers for current and future opportunities with leading organisations. If you're passionate about automation, cloud technologies, and bringing development and operations together, we'd love to connect with you.
Duties & Responsibilities

Key Responsibilities:

Conduct and optimise cloud infrastructure to ensure scalability, reliability, and performance
Orchestrate CI/CD pipelines that enable smooth, efficient software delivery
Harmonise development and operations teams through DevOps best practices
Compose Infrastructure as Code solutions that deliver consistency and automation
Coordinate containerised environments using Docker and Kubernetes
Monitor the platform's rhythm through logging, alerting, and observability tools
Fine-tune system performance and proactively resolve infrastructure bottlenecks
Champion security, resilience, and operational excellence across the environment
 
Job Experience and Skills Required:

Conduct cloud platforms across Azure, AWS, or GCP
Orchestrate seamless CI/CD performances from development to deployment
Compose Infrastructure as Code using Terraform, ARM, or CloudFormation
Lead container orchestration with Docker and Kubernetes
Automate repetitive movements through PowerShell, Python, and Bash scripting
Keep every section in tune through monitoring, logging, and observability practices
Ensure security and compliance remain in perfect harmony with DevSecOps principles
Fine-tune reliability, scalability, and performance to deliver a flawless production performance
 
This is an opportunity to join our talent pool and be considered for future opportunities with leading organisations across South Africa.
