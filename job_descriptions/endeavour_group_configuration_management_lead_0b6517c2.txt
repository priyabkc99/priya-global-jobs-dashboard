About The Role

Configuration Management Lead

As the Configuration Management Lead, you will take full ownership of the governance, accuracy, and integrity of our Configuration Management Database (CMDB) and supporting processes within the ServiceNow platform. Reporting to the Senior Manager – Service Management, you will act as the technical subject matter expert driving the maturity of our ITSM, ITOM and Event Management capabilities, including Discovery and Service Mapping. This vital role bridges technology and business outcomes by ensuring configuration data is reliable, integrated with observability tools like Dynatrace, and optimised to enable seamless decision-making, incident resolution, and automation across our enterprise environment.


Love what you do and leave your mark
Hybrid ways of working - Sydney or Melbourne location
Strategic Enterprise Influence: Own and elevate the ServiceNow CMDB Event integration and ITOM roadmap within a dynamic, large-scale technology ecosystem.
Modern Integration Ecosystem: Drive automation and observability by connecting ServiceNow with modern enterprise cloud tools and monitoring platforms.


Sounds good? Read on.

Here is a taster of what you can expect in this role:


CMDB Governance Leadership: Establish and maintain robust configuration management frameworks, ensuring CMDB data accuracy, CI relationship mapping, and alignment with CSDM and ITIL standards.
ServiceNow Platform Mastery: Serve as the subject matter expert across ServiceNow ITSM and ITOM modules, managing Service Catalog items, workflows, and platform enhancements.
Discovery Service Mapping: Oversee ServiceNow Discovery, Service Mapping, credential management, and MID server administration to build a truly service-aware CMDB.
Observability Event Integration: Configure seamless integrations between ServiceNow and external monitoring tools like Dynatrace, establishing automated event management and incident workflows.
Cross-Functional Team Collaboration: Partner with enterprise architecture, cyber security, and service delivery teams to drive CMDB adoption, data health reporting, and continuous process improvement.


What do we need from you

Now Let’s Talk About You


Extensive Platform Experience: 8+ years of hands-on experience administering or managing ServiceNow, with a core focus on ITSM, ITOM, CMDB, and CSDM structures.
Technical Integration Expertise: Strong background in ServiceNow Discovery, Service Mapping, MID server setup, and API-based integrations (REST/SOAP, JavaScript).
Certified Industry Qualifications: Relevant ITIL (v3 or ITIL4) certifications alongside formal ServiceNow credentials (e.g., CIS-ITSM, CIS-Discovery, or ITOM Implementation Specialist) highly regarded.
Data Governance Capability: Proven ability to establish reconciliation controls, build health dashboards, and manage enhancement backlogs effectively.
Stakeholder Engagement Focus: Excellent communication and relationship management skills with a proven track record of collaborating across complex enterprise environments.
