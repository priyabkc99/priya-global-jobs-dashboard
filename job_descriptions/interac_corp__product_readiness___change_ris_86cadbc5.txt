Who You Will Work With:

The vacant Product Readiness & Change Risk, Lead role will report to the Leader, Quality & Certification CoE. This role is responsible for ensuring that all product and technology changes meet enterprise standards for delivery readiness, risk management, and operational acceptance within the Enterprise Delivery Framework (EDF). This role plays a critical leadership function in embedding consistent, risk-based governance and gating discipline across the delivery lifecycle, ensuring that changes are fit-for-purpose, traceable, and production-ready before release. The role directly supports Interac’s objective to strengthen delivery quality and reduce production risk by shifting from judgement-based, late-stage controls to structured, evidence-driven readiness and risk management practices.

What You Will Do:


Own and operationalize readiness gating across EDF, with particular focus on:
Build readiness
SIT / performance readiness
Production approval
Operational acceptance


Ensure clear exit criteria and evidence are defined, measurable, and consistently executed across teams
Drive standardization of readiness artefacts, checklists, and evidence models to support auditability and traceability
Establish and lead a proactive, lifecycle-based approach to change risk management, ensuring:
Risks are identified at intake and throughout the lifecycle (not just at release stage)
Consistent risk evaluation across products and initiatives


Implement and mature initiative evaluation and risk scoring approaches to determine appropriate governance rigor and gates
Partner with Risk, Security, and Product teams to align operational, regulatory, and technology risks
Partner with Technology Operations and Product teams to
Ensure consistent application of readiness standards
Clarify accountability between Product, Engineering, and Operations
Embed readiness requirements early in the delivery lifecycle (not just pre-release), addressing current late-stage validation gaps
Provide guidance, frameworks, and tooling direction to enable operational teams
Define and standardize enterprise operational readiness expectations across all products and delivery teams,
Establish repeatable, enterprise-wide readiness practices that shift the organization from decentralized, inconsistent readiness approaches to standardized, evidence-based operational assurance
Define delivery KPIs (e.g., predictability, cycle time, milestone achievement).
Drive data-informed improvements across delivery practices.
Act as a change leader for EDF adoption. Drive awareness, training, and coaching across squads and product teams


What You Bring:


Bachelor’s Degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, or related discipline.
PMP, PMI-ACP, SAFe Agilist certifications are considered an asset.
7+ years of experience in software delivery lifecycle governance, release management, or operational readiness
Proven track record operating in complex, regulated environments (e.g., financial services, payments)
Experience implementing or supporting enterprise delivery frameworks, gating models, or quality control practices
Demonstrated ability to work across Product, Engineering, and Operations within a matrixed organization
Strong understanding of end-to-end SDLC, including requirements, build, testing, release, and operational acceptance
Expertise in change risk management, including early risk identification, classification, and mitigation
Experience defining and applying operational readiness standards (e.g., monitoring, resiliency, incident readiness)
Ability to design and implement evidence-based controls, gating criteria, and traceability practices
Skilled in establishing enterprise standards and improving consistency across diverse delivery models
