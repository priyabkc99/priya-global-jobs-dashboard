About The Job

As a DevOps Engineer, you will contribute to enterprise-scale technology initiatives aligned with Willsmarg Technologies LLC’s product engineering, cloud, data, AI, security, and delivery ecosystem.

Responsibilities


Design, build, and support enterprise-grade solutions aligned to the Cloud & DevOps function.
Collaborate with product, engineering, QA, cloud, security, and delivery teams to execute project milestones.
Write clean, maintainable, secure, and scalable implementation artifacts based on role expectations.
Participate in design reviews, sprint planning, issue resolution, documentation, and release readiness.
Support continuous improvement across engineering standards, delivery quality, and operational maturity.


Minimum Qualifications


3–5 Years of relevant experience in enterprise technology environments.
Strong problem-solving, communication, documentation, and collaboration skills.
Hands-on experience with core tools, platforms, or technologies listed for this role.
Ability to work in structured delivery environments with accountability and quality focus.
Understanding of enterprise application delivery, cloud systems, data, QA, security, or support practices.


Preferred Qualifications


Experience working on enterprise product platforms, SaaS systems, or large-scale business applications.
Exposure to Agile delivery, CI/CD practices, cloud-native systems, governance, or compliance workflows.
Ability to work across distributed USA and India delivery teams.
Strong ownership mindset with attention to quality, timelines, stakeholder communication, and documentation.


Education

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, Electronics, Data Science, Cybersecurity, or a related discipline preferred.

Certifications


Microsoft Azure Fundamentals / Administrator
AWS Cloud Practitioner
DevOps or Kubernetes certification preferred


Benefits


Competitive compensation aligned to India market standards.
Learning, certification, and technical growth support.
Structured project exposure across enterprise engineering and delivery environments.
Hybrid or remote flexibility depending on project and role requirements.
Career growth path across engineering, QA, cloud, data, support, and delivery tracks.
