Job Description

You will establish and continuously maintain the authoritative asset register and CMDB across the full asset lifecycle and across all asset classes — hardware, software, cloud, cryptographic, open-source and third-party components — and drive the people, process and tooling that keep it current. The role is accountable for asset data quality, reconciliation and governance, enabling effective service management, risk management, cost optimisation and informed decision-making. Beyond building the capability, you will run and sustain it as an ongoing service.

Job Responsibilities


Lead the Bank's Service Asset & Configuration Management (SACM) strategy and roadmap
Establish and maintain a single authoritative inventory of Service assets across hardware, software, cryptographic, open-source and third-party components
Drive asset discovery, data reconciliation and remediation to improve asset visibility and data quality
Oversee asset lifecycle governance, including onboarding, maintenance and decommissioning processes
Lead automation initiatives and support the implementation and optimisation of ServiceNow CMDB / ITAM capabilities
Establish governance metrics, KRIs and reporting to support audit and regulatory requirements
Partner with Technology, Information Security, Enterprise Architecture and business stakeholders to strengthen asset management practices across the Bank.

Job Requirements


Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering or a related discipline
At least 8 years of experience in Service Asset Management, Configuration Management, CMDB or related disciplines
Strong knowledge of ITIL SACM, asset lifecycle management, data reconciliation and governance frameworks
Hands-on experience with ServiceNow ITAM, CMDB and Discovery; experience with Device42 or ManageEngine is advantageous
Familiarity with Power Platform tools such as Power Automate, Dataverse and Power BI
Good understanding of technology risk, audit and regulatory requirements within a financial services environment
Strong stakeholder management, communication and leadership skills, with the ability to drive cross-functional initiatives.
