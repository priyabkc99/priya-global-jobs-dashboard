WHAT IS ON OFFER




Alfred H Knight has an exciting opportunity for an Agile Delivery Manager to join our team in Prescot.




This is a brand-new, pivotal role within our IT function during an exciting period of digital transformation, modernization, and business growth. As a trusted global leader spanning five generations, we combine our rich heritage with modern technology to drive continuous innovation across our international operations.




Key Role Highlights & Responsibilities:

Work Life Balance: Enjoy a great work-life balance with 3 days on-site at our Prescot head office and 2 days working from home.
Global Impact: Work as part of a collaborative, forward-thinking global team shaping our ongoing IT strategy.
Scrum & Agile Leadership: Facilitate key Scrum events (Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum, Retrospectives) and coach teams on Agile best practices.
Release & Delivery Management: Plan, coordinate, and lead software releases for all in-house applications across the full lifecycle.
Cross-Functional Bridge: Act as the key link between technical development teams, QA, IT Support, and business partners to remove blockers and drive delivery.
Continuous Improvement: Research and implement new configuration and delivery methodologies to automate and enhance deployment speed and quality.




ABOUT US




Alfred H Knight is a totally independent, family owned business spanning five generations. A global network of strategically placed offices and laboratories enable global trade by providing independent inspection, analysis and consultancy services to the metals and minerals, solid fuels and agriculture industries.




We have honed and carefully crafted our reputation. Delivering knowledge and professionalism in all aspects of weighing, sampling and analysis. We thrive by continuing to re-invest in our facilities, technology and people. Click here to find out more about AHK.




DO YOU HAVE WHAT IT TAKES?




To be successful at Alfred H Knight you will need to display the following:




REQUIRED KNOWLEDGE AND WORK EXPERIENCE




Essential

Strong understanding of the Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC), including agile and hybrid delivery models (Scrum, Kanban, SAFe).
Familiarity with DevOps practices, CI/CD pipelines, and tooling such as Jenkins, Git, Azure DevOps, or GitLab.
Knowledge of release and change management processes, including integration with ITIL functions and governance requirements.
Basic understanding of software architecture concepts, APIs, cloud infrastructure (AWS/Azure), and microservices.




Required Competencies

Outstanding communication, facilitation, negotiation, and coaching skills.
Strong collaborative and problem-solving skills, with the ability to bring fresh, creative ideas to challenges.
Proactive, curious, and solution-oriented with a drive to challenge the status quo and exemplify Agile values.
High level of integrity and professionalism.




Required Work Experience

Proven experience working in an Agile Delivery role (e.g., Scrum Master, Agile Delivery Manager, Software Delivery Manager, Product Owner).
Demonstrable experience facilitating meetings across multiple organisational levels.
Strong experience managing stakeholder expectations and navigating complex stakeholder scenarios.




Required Qualifications

Certified Scrum Master qualification (preferred).
Degree educated (desirable).
