The Opportunity

As the Senior Quality Engineer, the Senior Quality Engineer is responsible for activities related to the design, control, qualification, specification and corrective and preventative action associated with manufacturing in the plants they support. They lead the design planning efforts and track completion for complex projects, documentation for projects to support change control policies and lead risk management activities. They also get involved and either lead or support the more complex CAPA activities.

What You’ll Do


Understands design control processes and lead projects to ensure compliance with the process.
Execute qualification/validation activities to support engineering projects.
Analyze complex interdisciplinary problems and identifies their impact, establishes probabilities, draws conclusions reflecting broad business needs, understands organizational and financial impact of recommendations, e.g., applies regulatory requirements and recommends best course of action, sets complex design and process requirements.
Ability to juggle multiple priorities, establishes workable and trackable schedules.
Strong technical writing skills to capture complex technical requirements, cause/effect and risks in design plans, qualifications, and investigations.
Strong verbal communications to work with engineers, quality and global operations team members to ensure compliance to division policies.
Position is responsible for execution of design control and CAPA activities.


Requirements


Desired, not essential, Bachelor’s Degree in Business, Science, Technical or related field and/or an equivalent combination of education and work experience.
Minimum 5 years in a similar position or in food manufacturing; Working knowledge of applicable regulations, compliance requirements, quality systems, and engineering / quality principles as relates to applicable manufacturing environment, preferably consumable goods.
Applies, quality-focused thinking and strong project management skills to plan and conduct effective investigations, delivering outcomes in a timely manner.
Demonstrates high attention to detail to ensure accuracy within Quality System documentation, alongside clear written and verbal communication for concise reporting and stakeholder engagement at all levels.
Works independently with confidence, managing responsibilities and ensuring consistent, reliable outputs.


Location

This position is based in Pokeno, Waikato.
