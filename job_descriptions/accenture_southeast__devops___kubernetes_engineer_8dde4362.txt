We’re looking for experienced DevOps & Kubernetes Engineers to join our growing technology team. In this role, you’ll help design, build, and maintain scalable CI/CD pipelines and containerized platforms that support complex, enterprise-grade systems. You’ll work closely with cloud, infrastructure, and development teams to automate deployments, improve observability, and ensure reliability across hybrid and cloud environments.

Key Responsibilities:



Develop and optimize CI/CD pipelines (Jenkins, GitLab, GitHub Actions)

Manage containerization and orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes, OpenShift)

Automate infrastructure provisioning using Terraform, Ansible, or CloudFormation

Collaborate with developers, infra, and cloud teams to ensure reliable delivery



Basic Qualifications:



Hands-on experience in DevOps, CI/CD, or Kubernetes administration

Strong scripting skills (Python, Bash, Groovy)

Exposure to cloud environments (AWS, Azure, or GCP)



Preferred Skills:



Experience in monitoring and observability (Grafana, Prometheus, ELK)

Familiar with networking and security principles
