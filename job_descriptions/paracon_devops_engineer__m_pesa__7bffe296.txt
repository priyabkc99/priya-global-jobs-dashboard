DevOps Engineer SAS AML Platform

Location: Midrand, Gauteng

Reporting Line: Manager Platform Engineering







Job Summary

We are seeking a highly skilled DevOps Engineer to support the M-Pesa platform, with a strong focus on the SAS Anti-Money Laundering (AML) solution. The role involves enabling platform reliability, automating deployments, and supporting the application layer to ensure seamless financial crime detection operations. You will work closely with engineering, security, and vendor teams to maintain a secure, scalable, and highly available AML platform.




Key Responsibilities

Design, implement, and manage CI/CD pipelines for SAS AML and supporting application layers
Support the enablement, configuration, and optimisation of the SAS AML platform within M-Pesa
Collaborate with internal teams and external partners (e.g., SAS, Binary) to integrate and stabilise application components
Automate infrastructure provisioning and environment setup using Infrastructure as Code (IaC) tools
Manage cloud and on-premises hybrid environments hosting AML workloads
Monitor system health, performance, and availability using enterprise monitoring tools
Ensure platform compliance with regulatory, security, and financial governance standards
Troubleshoot production incidents, perform root cause analysis, and implement preventive measures
Manage releases, patches, and upgrades for the SAS AML platform
Implement DevSecOps practices, including vulnerability scanning and secure configuration management
Maintain operational documentation, runbooks, and standard operating procedures




Required Skills & Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Engineering, or equivalent experience
3–7+ years of experience in DevOps, Site Reliability Engineering, or platform engineering
Experience working with financial services platforms or regulated environments (preferably mobile money or banking)
Strong experience with CI/CD tools (Jenkins, GitLab, Azure DevOps)
Hands-on experience with Linux/Unix system administration
Knowledge of SAS platform administration (preferably SAS AML or SAS Viya)
Experience with containerization technologies (Docker, Kubernetes)
Proficiency in scripting (Bash, Python, PowerShell)
Experience with Infrastructure as Code tools (Terraform, Ansible, ARM templates)
Familiarity with monitoring tools (Splunk, Grafana, Prometheus, or similar)
Understanding of networking, APIs, and enterprise integration patterns

Preferred Skills

Experience with M-Pesa or mobile financial services platforms
Knowledge of Anti-Money Laundering (AML), fraud detection, or financial crime systems
Experience supporting SAS solutions in production environments
Exposure to microservices and event-driven architectures
Cloud certifications (Azure, AWS, or GCP)
Experience with database platforms (Oracle, PostgreSQL, or SAS data environments)

Key Competencies

Strong problem-solving and analytical thinking
Ability to work under pressure in mission-critical environments
Strong stakeholder management and collaboration skills
Attention to detail and commitment to system reliability and security
Continuous improvement mindset and automation-first approach
