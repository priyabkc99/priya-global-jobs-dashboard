Responsibilities

Deploy software applications across development, staging, and production environments
Integrate applications with external systems, data sources, and infrastructure services
Diagnose and resolve deployment and integration issues
Build, configure, deploy, and troubleshoot containerised applications
Manage configuration across different environments and maintain CI/CD pipelines
Create deployment scripts and automate repetitive deployment tasks
Work with infrastructure teams to resolve network, firewall, and connectivity issues




Requirements

Experience deploying applications across development, staging, and production environments, including integration with external systems and infrastructure services
Strong Linux and server administration skills, with ability to troubleshoot using system logs and shell scripting
Hands on experience with Docker and containerised applications; exposure to Kubernetes and Helm is a plus
Familiar with Git/GitLab, CI/CD pipelines, and automating deployment scripts across environments
Good understanding of networking fundamentals (TCP/IP, ports, firewalls, DNS) and ability to work with infra teams on connectivity issues
QA background is a plus
