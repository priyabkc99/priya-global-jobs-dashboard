Job Description

The Technical Writer will be responsible for creating and maintaining support material such as Product Manuals, procedures for O&G tools from Content Management tool. As a member of a small, dynamic documentation team and an integral part of the enterprise organization within an agile environment, he or she will make sure that instructional guides and reference manuals are clear enough for new users and detailed enough for advanced ones.

Essential Responsibilities


Generate technical content from existing documentation and tool manuals of O&G tools
Strategize, Plan and execute content level and description
Collaborate with SME and AMO technicians and suggest Event-based Maintenance (EBM) strategies instead of Levels of Maintenance (LoM) based on tools usage and repair cycles
Interact with technicians (end-users) and update chunks for improved productivity
Write engaging content from AMO's technician point of view
