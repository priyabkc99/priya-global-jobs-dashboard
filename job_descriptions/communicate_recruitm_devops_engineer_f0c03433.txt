Cloud DevOps Engineer (Senior)

Johannesburg | Full-Time | Hybrid after probation




Join a High-Growth Technology Team

We are looking for an experienced and hands-on Cloud DevOps Engineer to help build, optimize, and maintain the cloud infrastructure powering a rapidly growing SaaS platform.




This is an exciting opportunity for someone who enjoys creating reliable, scalable, and secure systems while working closely with software engineers and data teams. You'll play a critical role in automating deployments, improving system performance, and ensuring a seamless development and operational experience.




If you're passionate about cloud technologies, infrastructure automation, and solving complex technical challenges, we'd love to hear from you.




What You'll Do

Infrastructure & Cloud Management

Design, implement, and manage scalable, highly available cloud infrastructure.
Optimize cloud environments for reliability, performance, and cost efficiency.
Manage and maintain AWS services including ECS, EC2, RDS, S3, CloudFront, Lambda, and related services.

CI/CD & Automation

Build, maintain, and enhance CI/CD pipelines using GitHub Actions, Jenkins, or similar platforms.
Automate deployment processes and operational tasks to increase efficiency and reduce manual intervention.
Champion DevOps best practices across development workflows.

Containerisation & Orchestration

Manage containerized applications using Docker.
Support orchestration platforms such as Kubernetes.
Ensure consistency across development, testing, and production environments.

Monitoring & Reliability

Implement and maintain monitoring, alerting, and logging solutions.
Create dashboards and define key operational metrics.
Proactively identify and resolve performance bottlenecks and reliability risks.

Security & Governance

Apply cloud security best practices and infrastructure hardening techniques.
Assist in implementing firewalls, access controls, and intrusion detection solutions.
Ensure infrastructure complies with security and operational standards.

Database & Disaster Recovery

Support PostgreSQL database environments and contribute to NoSQL initiatives where appropriate.
Design and maintain backup, recovery, and disaster recovery strategies.
Ensure critical systems are protected and recoverable.

Collaboration & Documentation

Work closely with development teams to troubleshoot production issues and improve release processes.
Create and maintain clear technical documentation for infrastructure, systems, and procedures.
Contribute to a culture of continuous improvement and operational excellence.




What We're Looking For

Qualifications

Degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, Mathematics, or a related field preferred.
AWS certifications advantageous.




Technical Skills

Strong experience with AWS cloud services.
Solid knowledge of Docker and Kubernetes.
Experience with CI/CD tools such as GitHub Actions or Jenkins.
Hands-on experience with Infrastructure as Code tools such as Terraform, CloudFormation, or Ansible.
Familiarity with monitoring platforms including Prometheus, Grafana, and CloudWatch.
Experience administering PostgreSQL databases.
Proficiency with Git and GitHub.
Strong scripting skills using Python, Bash, Ruby, or similar languages.
Experience integrating APIs, systems, and workflows to support automation and operational efficiency.




Experience & Competencies

Strong understanding of networking, cloud architecture, and security principles.
Experience supporting high-availability and scalable production environments.
Excellent troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.
Strong attention to detail and operational discipline.
Effective communication and collaboration skills.
Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, technology-driven environment.




Nice to Have

Experience contributing to open-source projects or participating in the DevOps community.
Exposure to hybrid cloud environments.
Previous experience within a SaaS or startup environment.




Why Join Us?

Work on impactful cloud and infrastructure projects.
Collaborate with talented engineers in a fast-paced environment.
Influence architecture, automation, and operational best practices.
Opportunity to make a significant impact within a growing technology business.




Ready to build reliable, scalable cloud infrastructure and take ownership of DevOps excellence?




Apply today!
