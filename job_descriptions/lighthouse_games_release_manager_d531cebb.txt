Lighthouse Games is a new AAA studio in Leamington Spa. We are defined by our pedigree of talent and serious ambition to build a new best-in-class game. When you join us at Lighthouse, you will be able to create the best work of your career in an environment that feels truly collaborative. You will learn from the best and work alongside the future greats of the gaming industry. 

As a Release Manager, you will play a pivotal role within the QA department, taking ownership of the end-to-end release process across all target platforms. You will coordinate build cadence, milestone readiness, platform certification submissions and go-live logistics, working closely with the QA Lead on test coverage, exit criteria and release-gate decisions. This is a high-visibility, cross-functional role that requires close collaboration with Production, Engineering, Build Engineering, Art, Design, Audio and QA.



Requirements


Coordinate build packaging, patch scheduling, and hotfix deployment processes
Maintain the branching and build-promotion strategy in partnership with Build Engineering
Own the build release schedule, ensuring builds are packaged, promoted and distributed in line with agreed milestones and release timelines
Coordinate build handovers between Engineering, Build Engineering, QA and Production, ensuring all teams have clear visibility
Ensure release builds meet agreed readiness criteria before promotion to QA, certification or production environments
Track build stability and identify potential risks or blockers
Manage build versioning and ensure consistent naming, labelling and tracking across platforms and environments
Coordinate patch and hotfix timelines, ensuring appropriate testing, approvals and deployment windows are agreed with relevant teams
Work closely with Build Engineering to identify and resolve build pipeline, packaging or deployment issues that could impact delivery
Communicate build status, deployment timelines, blockers and changes to relevant stakeholders in a clear and timely manner


Desirable Experience 

Previous experience working as a Release Manager
Experience managing complex releases across multiple platforms
Familiarity with platform certification processes and requirements
Strong understanding of software development lifecycles, build pipelines, branching strategies and deployment processes
Experience working closely with Build Engineering, DevOps or technical teams to manage build promotion and release pipelines
Knowledge of QA methodologies, test coverage, release criteria and quality gates
Experience using source control and build management systems in a production environment
Experience working within Agile development environments and coordinating multiple teams against fixed milestones
Experience producing release dashboards, status reports, risk registers and executive-level reporting
Strong understanding of release risk management, including technical, content, certification and schedule risks
Experience working with external stakeholders, platform holders or third-party partners
A strong understanding of game development workflows and the relationship between Production, Engineering, Art, Design, Audio and QA
