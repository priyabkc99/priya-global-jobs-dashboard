About the Role




We're looking for an experienced Release Manager to own and drive the end-to-end release and deployment lifecycle for Juspay's core Express Checkout product — a high-scale, high-availability payments system built on microservices. This role sits at the intersection of Engineering, DevOps, Product, and QA, and is expected to be hands-on: driving deployments, monitoring rollouts, running experiments, and ensuring every release is validated, well-coordinated, and delivered reliably at scale.




The ideal candidate combines strong technical depth in deployment engineering and observability with proven experience managing complex, high-frequency releases across many independently deployable services.




What You'll Do




Own end-to-end deployment management for all microservices powering Express Checkout, across multiple engineering teams.
Define release strategy, timelines, quality gates, readiness criteria, and go/no-go processes.
Design and manage progressive rollout strategies (canary, blue-green, staged rollouts) and run A/B tests to validate feature and performance changes before full production release.
Partner closely with Engineering, QA, Product, and DevOps to plan, sequence, and execute releases across interdependent services.
Drive functional, regression, integration, API, performance, and end-to-end testing across releases.
Build and monitor release/deployment dashboards (Grafana or equivalent) to track health, latency, error rates, and rollout progress in real time.
Query and analyze release, performance, and product metrics using SQL, ClickHouse, and BigQuery to inform go/no-go decisions and post-release health checks.
Track and manage release risks, dependencies, blockers, and mitigation plans.
Coordinate production deployments, release validation, rollback plans, and post-release monitoring.
Establish and monitor release quality metrics, including defect leakage, release stability, automation coverage, deployment success rates, and A/B test outcomes.
Identify bottlenecks in the deployment pipeline and drive improvements in tooling, automation, testing, and CI/CD workflows (Jenkins and related systems).
Lead release retrospectives and work with teams to implement corrective and preventive actions.
Maintain release documentation, deployment checklists, release notes, and governance processes.
Act as the central point of coordination during critical or high-impact releases, including incident response and rollback decisions.




What We're Looking For




5–7 years of experience in Release Management, DevOps, Deployment Engineering, or a closely related engineering function, ideally in a microservices environment.
Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
Hands-on experience owning production deployments for microservices at scale — not just coordinating release schedules.
Practical experience designing and running A/B tests / experiments as part of feature rollout and release validation.
Working proficiency in SQL, and hands-on experience querying analytical data stores such as ClickHouse and BigQuery.
Hands-on experience with Grafana (or equivalent observability tools) for building dashboards and monitoring production releases.
Hands-on experience with CI/CD and release tooling such as Jenkins, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or similar platforms.
Deep understanding of SDLC, testing methodologies, release management, and software quality practices.
Strong technical understanding of APIs, distributed systems, CI/CD pipelines, version control, and production environments.
Strong stakeholder management skills with the ability to drive decisions and coordinate teams during high-pressure releases.
Excellent analytical, problem-solving, planning, and communication skills.
Experience working in Agile/product development environments with frequent and iterative releases.




Good to Have




Experience in fintech, payments, or checkout/transaction systems specifically — familiarity with payment flows, PSP integrations, and checkout funnel metrics.
Experience with test automation frameworks and quality engineering practices.
Knowledge of performance testing, reliability testing, and chaos/resilience testing.
Familiarity with Jira, Confluence, incident management, and engineering productivity tools.




Key Skills




Release Management • Microservices Deployment • A/B Testing & Experimentation • CI/CD (Jenkins) • Grafana & Observability • SQL • ClickHouse • BigQuery • Test Strategy & Automation • Risk & Dependency Management • Stakeholder Management • Incident & Rollback Management • Agile/Scrum




What Success Looks Like




Microservice releases for Express Checkout are deployed predictably, on schedule, and with minimal production issues.
A/B tests and staged rollouts are used consistently to de-risk feature and performance changes before full release.
Clear quality gates and release readiness criteria are consistently followed across teams.
Engineering and Product stakeholders have real-time visibility into release progress, risks, and dependencies via dashboards.
Production defects and release-related incidents are continuously reduced through better testing, monitoring, and automation.
The deployment process becomes faster, more reliable, and increasingly automated.
