A well established company in Dublin are hiring for a new Change Manager that has become available due to continued growth within this company. This is a new role within an SME.




About the role:

Own and coordinate the change management process across IT and business teams
Plan, schedule, and oversee software releases and deployments
Manage risk assessments, approvals, and communication for changes
Work closely with development, QA, and operations teams to ensure smooth, low-disruption releases
Maintain change and release documentation and reporting

What we're looking for:

Proven experience in change and/or release management
Strong stakeholder management and communication skills
Comfortable working in a fast-paced environment with competing priorities
Based in or willing to work from Dublin
