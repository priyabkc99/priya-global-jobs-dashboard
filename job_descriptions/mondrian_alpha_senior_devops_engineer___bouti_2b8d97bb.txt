Hedge Fund Technology Search | Mondrian Alpha

My client, a technology-first hedge fund, is seeking a Senior DevOps Engineer to join their London team.




The successful individual will be responsible for designing, automating, and optimising cloud infrastructure and application delivery pipelines within a on prem and cloud environment. You will partner closely with engineering teams to enable scalable, secure, and resilient solutions, with a strong focus on automation and continuous improvement.




You should have deep hands-on expertise across containerisation, infrastructure as code, and CI/CD, with a solid grounding in modern DevOps practices. Experience with Python/Java/Go/C# is highly desirable.




Ideal candidates should have experience with the below:

Kubernetes experience - K8s
Infrastructure as code using Terraform; familiarity with Bicep is a plus
Building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines using tools such as Tekton and Azure DevOps
Docker and containerisation best practices
Git-based version control systems including Bitbucket and GitHub




My client has committed to paying a strong increase on any base salary and is known for offering highly competitive bonuses. In addition, they provide an excellent benefits package and a collaborative, high-performance environment where engineers are empowered to make an impact.




Apply now by following the link below or send your resume directly to harvey.gilbert@mondrian-alpha.com
