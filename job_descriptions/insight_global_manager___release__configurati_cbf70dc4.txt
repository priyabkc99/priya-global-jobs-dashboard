Insight Global is seeking an experienced technology leader to establish, manage, and continuously improve enterprise Release Management, Configuration Management, and Environment Management capabilities. This role serves as the central point of accountability for software release governance, deployment coordination, configuration control, CMDB accuracy, environment lifecycle management, operational readiness, and continuous delivery practices across a complex enterprise technology landscape. The ideal candidate has successfully led enterprise release and deployment practices in large, regulated organizations. The successful candidate will partner across Development, DevOps, QA, Infrastructure, Cloud Engineering, Security, Operations, Architecture, and Business teams to ensure technology changes are delivered safely, efficiently, and with minimal business disruption.




Day-to-day:

Govern complex release calendars across multiple teams and platforms
Establish mature release management processes and controls
Drive CI/CD adoption and deployment automation
Improve change success rates and reduce deployment failures
Manage CMDB governance and configuration integrity
Oversee non-production and production environments across cloud and hybrid platforms
Coordinate application, infrastructure, and platform releases
Improve operational stability while accelerating delivery velocity
Communicate effectively with executives, technical teams, and business stakeholders




Requirements:

8+ years of experience in Release Management, DevOps, IT Delivery, Environment Management, and Configuration Management
3+ years of people leadership experience
Experience leading enterprise-wide release activities
Experience supporting large-scale application portfolios
Experience within cloud, hybrid, and on-premises environments
Excellent communication skills




Nice to have:

Insurance experience
Experience operating within ITIL-based organizations
Experience building processes and governance from the ground up




Pay: 60-70

Vacancy: New Vacancy (New Role)
