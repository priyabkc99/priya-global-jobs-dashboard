DevOps Automation Platform Engineer

An extraordinarily talented group of individuals work together every day to drive TNS' success, from both professional and personal perspectives. Come join the excellence!

Overview

TNS Automation Development is looking for a passionate, motivated Platform Engineer to join our global team of Automation Developers and mature our DevOps processes and automation infrastructure. The ideal candidate will have experience mapping, building, implementing, and maintaining platform management plans, great communication skills, and a passion for network automation. We’d prefer someone with 10-15 years in IT who adds a wide set of skills to our own.

The Platform Engineer Will:


Help us shape our DevOps Automation and AI journey;
Own our GitLab foundation including
Standards around repo creation, pipelines, and deployment.
Design, build, maintain, and optimize CI/CD pipelines.
Standardize and coordinate unit, integration, and security testing.
Plan, coordinate, publicize, and monitor application releases and updates.
Maintain the health and stability of our automation and orchestration applications, plugins, pipelines, and functions.
Define and document environment and infrastructure requirements, in particular around cloud, virtualized, and physical deployments.
Define and document patterns and policies for safe use and deployment of AI-assisted automation, including but not limited to infrastructure, architecture, and deployment standards.
Work with Security and Risk teams to continuously assess our security posture.

The position will require interaction with internal teams (Global Systems, NOC, and stakeholders in the Business Units) for requirements gathering and deployment coordination.

Experience - Required


SDLC and CI/CD pipelines
Release management and best practices
Software development and engineering
Network and software automation
Monitoring platforms (Netreo a plus)
Python
AWS
Ansible / Terraform
Jinja / Django
Docker and containerization in general


Experience – Desired


Nautobot / NetBox or other network management tool
Network engineering
Experience in international environments


Education


BA/BS in Computer Science or IT related field (or equivalent experience)
DevOps certified (AWS or Azure)
