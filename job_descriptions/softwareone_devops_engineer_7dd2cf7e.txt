Passionate about improving cloud deployment, automation, and operational efficiency for customers running workloads on AWS and Azure?

Motivated to collaborate within a high‑performing cloud services team focused on continuous improvement and customer impact?

Inspired to join a global organisation where innovation, teamwork, and cloud expertise shape real customer outcomes?

As our new DevOps Engineer, you will play a key role in delivering Cloud Platform Management (CPM) services to customers, helping improve deployment automation, reliability, and time‑to‑market. Working closely with internal teams and customers, you will contribute to cloud operations, DevOps practices, and ongoing service optimisation across modern cloud environments.

Key responsibilities will include

Support the delivery and execution of cloud and DevOps projects across customer environments
Identify opportunities to improve automation, efficiency, and overall service performance
Contribute to service innovation by proposing new features, enhancements, and operational improvements
Operate within change management processes to ensure stability and compliance across environments
Collaborate with internal teams and customers to maintain strong relationships and high service standards
Job Requirements
Experience managing cloud environments across AWS and/or Azure platforms
Experience with DevOps practices, CI/CD pipelines, and deployment automation
Strong understanding of backend technologies and cloud-based systems
Ability to deliver reliable technical solutions with a strong customer focus
Strong communication skills with the ability to collaborate effectively across teams and stakeholders
Demonstrated ability to take ownership, prioritise work effectively, and drive continuous improvement initiatives
Company description

 

SoftwareOne and Crayon have come together to form a global, AI-powered software and cloud solutions provider with a bold vision for the future. At the heart of our business is our people. Whether you're passionate about cloud, software, data, AI, or building meaningful client relationships, you’ll find a place to thrive here. Join us and be part of a purpose-driven culture where your ideas matter, your growth is supported, and your career can go global.
