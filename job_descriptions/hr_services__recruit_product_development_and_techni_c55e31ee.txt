Introduction

 a security company in the West Rand is looking for a product develooppment and Technical services support Manager to lead the technical lifecycle of Company products by managing product testing, validation, releases, technical support, customer technical engagement, and product improvement. The role ensures that all hardware, software, and firmware products meet customer expectations while providing technical leadership to internal teams, engineering partners, and key clients.

 

 

Duties & Responsibilities

Responsibilities:

Product Development and Lifecycle Management: Supports product development by contributing to product design, roadmap execution, technical development, change control, release planning and documentation, while driving product adoption across the business.
Product Testing and Quality Assurance: Develops and manages product testing and validation activities, including UAT, PAT, PoC deployments and field trials, while monitoring performance, investigating issues, conducting root cause analysis, and providing engineering feedback to support continuous product improvement and successful commercial release.
Technical Support Leadership: Leads and coordinates the Technical Support team by setting priorities, managing issue resolution and escalations, ensuring compliance with company procedures, supporting operational technical needs, approving after-hours work, and providing standby support for critical customer incidents.
Client Technical Support: Provides advanced technical support to strategic customers by assisting with deployments, resolving escalated issues, maintaining key stakeholder and supplier relationships, participating in operational meetings, supporting forensic investigations, and delivering expert product advice.
Product Rollout and Configuration Management: Manages firmware, software and hardware rollout projects across approximately 110,000 field devices, ensuring successful deployment, progress monitoring, configuration compliance, and the effective maintenance of demo, laboratory, testing and Proof of Concept environments.
Technical Training and Training Administration: Develops and delivers technical training, documentation and product knowledge resources, including training materials, installation manuals and technical specifications, while supporting client onboarding, internal capability development, product demonstrations and ongoing user education.
Supplier and Engineer Management: Manages engineering suppliers and third-party technology partners by monitoring performance, ensuring deliverables meet company standards, coordinating corrective actions, evaluating product compatibility, and participating in supplier development and continuous improvement initiatives.
Governance and Change Control: Coordinates product governance and change management processes by facilitating Change Control and Change Acceptance Board meetings, recording and tracking actions, following up on outstanding items, reporting progress to management, and maintaining governance documentation.
Quality Assurance: Conducts quality assurance inspections across operational installations, workshop assemblies and technical support activities to verify compliance with engineering standards, identify improvement opportunities, and promote continuous quality improvement.
System Administration: Administers and supports internal technical platforms by managing user accounts and permissions, maintaining system integrity, assisting with technical reporting, and providing administrative support to ensure effective platform operation.
Technical Sales Support: Supports sales and customer engagement activities by contributing to solution design, participating in technical discussions, reviewing customer requirements, recommending suitable products, and assisting with customer presentations.
Incident Management: Attends customer incident debriefings, analyses product performance, provides technical feedback, facilitates lessons-learned sessions, and recommends product enhancements to drive continuous improvement and operational excellence.

 

Desired Experience & Qualification

Requirements:

General
Diploma or Degree in Electronic Engineering, Computer Engineering, Information Technology, or a related technical field.
7–10 years' experience in technical product support or product development.
Experience managing technical teams.
Experience with hardware, software and firmware products.
Experience with enterprise customer support.
Experience in product testing and quality assurance.
Experience in project management.
Experience working with Engineering teams and suppliers.
 
Technical Knowledge:
Product & Technical Management: Product lifecycle management, firmware management, software testing, hardware diagnostics, networking, SQL databases, and Windows Server administration.
Analysis & Continuous Improvement: Root cause analysis, change management, quality assurance, troubleshooting, and technical problem-solving.
Project & Process Management: Project management, product release coordination, change control, technical documentation, and process governance.
Stakeholder Support & Training: Customer relationship management, technical training, knowledge transfer, user support, and cross-functional collaboration.
