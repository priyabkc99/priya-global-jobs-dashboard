SUMMARY

Are you a sharp writer with a knack for simplifying complex information? Step into a role where your words drive clarity, support innovation, and help secure exciting opportunities.

We’re seeking a Technical /Proposal Writer to create clear, engaging documentation and compelling bid responses. This role blends technical writing with proposal development, requiring precision, creativity, and strong collaboration skill




Our client is looking to employ a Technical / Proposal Writer.







RESPONSIBILITIES

Develop user-friendly technical documentation (guides, manuals, training materials)

Translate complex concepts into clear, accessible content

Write and support bid and proposal submissions (RFPs, RFIs, RFQs)

Ensure accuracy, consistency, and compliance across all documents

Maintain and improve documentation and proposal libraries

Collaborate with subject matter experts and cross-functional tea




MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS

Matric / Grade 12 is essential

Bachelor’s degree in a technical discipline (e.g., Electrical, Mechanical, Computer, Civil Engineering) or equivalent technical experience

3 years Technical Writer / Proposal Writer/ similar experience

Experience using Google Workspace, Microsoft Office, Adobe Acrobat, Canva and other documentation tools

Proven ability to simplify technical concepts for various audiences.

Experience producing documentation for both hardware and software (including SaaS products)







Applications can be sent to marchelle.carelse@kelly.co.za
