Senior Automation & DevOps Engineer
Network Engineering
I'm interested
Reference: NES027885-KM-1

Our client is seeking a Senior Automation & DevOps Engineer to play a strategic role in transforming our engineering development environment. This position is ideal for a highly skilled engineer with expertise in Requirements Management, DevOps practices, CI/CD automation, and software lifecycle governance within complex product development environments.
 
The successful candidate will drive automation initiatives, improve engineering processes, and support compliance with industry standards while working closely with software, hardware, systems, and quality engineering teams.
Duties & Responsibilities
Key Responsibilities

Lead the implementation and improvement of requirements management practices across engineering projects.
Establish and maintain requirement traceability from concept through verification and validation.
Develop and manage CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins, Bitbucket, Artifactory, and related DevOps tooling.
Drive Agile and DevOps adoption across multidisciplinary engineering teams.
Configure and manage development infrastructure, user authentication systems, and deployment environments.
Design dashboards and reporting solutions to monitor engineering performance and project metrics.
Support cloud-based infrastructure deployments and containerized environments.
Maintain legacy development tools and facilitate migration to modern platforms.
Contribute to cybersecurity compliance and secure development practices.
Qualifications

BEng Electronic Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or equivalent.
Honours or Master's degree advantageous.
Experience & Technical Skills

8-10+ years in software, systems, or product development environments.
Strong experience in Requirements Management and DevOps.
Experience with safety-critical or regulated industries highly advantageous.
DOORS (Classic & Next Generation)
Jira, Jenkins, Bitbucket, Artifactory
SQL/MySQL
Python, Bash, PowerShell
Docker, Kubernetes, Podman
CI/CD pipelines and automation frameworks
RTCA DO-178, DO-254, ARP4754A knowledge preferred
