What we are looking for

Role: Automated Release Management

Experience Range: 5 – 10 Years

Location: Chennai/Hyderabad/Bangalore/Mumbai/Indore

Must Have:

1.Managing risks and resolving challenges that impact release scope, quality, and schedules

2.Planning release windows and cycles across portfolios, components

3.Managing relationships working on interrelated processes

4.Communicating crucial release plans and changes

5.Measuring and monitoring progress to achieve a timely software release within defined budgetary limits and defined quality standards

6.Coordinating processes between different teams (possibly in various locations)

Validating release notes

7.Deployment management of deliverables to the test environment particularly guaranteeing that the external dependent items are available during the test runtime
