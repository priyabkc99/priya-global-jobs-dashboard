Company Profile

RS2 offers secure payment services, payment software and managed services to clients in over 35 countries. The company upholds the highest industry standards and RS2’s in-house designed payments solution is the software of choice by many of the world’s leading and most innovative banks and financial institutions.




The Opportunity:

We are seeking a proactive Engineer to manage application deployments, cloud infrastructure, and CI/CD automation across production environments. Utilising Linux, AWS/Azure, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, and Bash, you will optimise system performance, monitor environments to ensure high availability, automate workflows, and resolve complex technical issues.




Key Responsibilities:

Installation & configuration of 3rd party middleware software as well as the company’s own application
Deployment of code releases on various environments, including production
Monitoring of data centres & environments to ensure minimal downtime
Designing continuous deployment & automation solutions
Researching & implementing ways of improving existing processes & workflows
Creating and maintaining documentation of procedures & systems
Assisting in performance optimization & IT security exercises




Requirements

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a similar, recognised qualification
Proficiency in scripting languages such as Bash
Hands-on experience with cloud computing platforms (preferably AWS and/or Azure)
Experience working with Linux servers in a production environment
Experience using Docker and/or Kubernetes
Experience with Terraform
Experience with Elastic Stack (beneficial, but not required)
Experience with troubleshooting and problem-solving situations
Fluency in written and spoken English
