Job Description


Improve release speed by standardizing and automating build/deploy/verify/rollback processes.
Maintain and enhance CI/CD pipelines and deployment scripts/tools.
Build and optimize monitoring, logging, and alerting for key systems.
Lead alert governance (severity, routing, de-duplication, noise reduction) and maintain runbooks/SOPs.
Perform production support, incident troubleshooting, RCA, and continuous improvement.
Ensure change records, access control, and audit-friendly operational documentation.


Job Requirements


3+ years in DevOps/SRE/Operations Engineering.
Strong Linux troubleshooting and operations skills.
Strong Shell scripting; proficient in Python automation.
Experience with CI/CD and production release management.
