Autoneum is globally leading in acoustic and thermal management for light and commercial vehicles. The Company develops and produces multifunctional, lightweight and sustainable components and systems for interior floor, interior trim as well as engine bay and underbody. Customers include almost all automobile manufacturers in Europe, North & South America, Asia and Africa. The Company with its head-quarters in Winterthur, Switzerland, is listed on the SIX Swiss Ex-change (ticker symbol AUTN).

Job Purpose

The Quality Engineer is responsible for ensuring customer and internal quality requirements are achieved throughout the manufacturing process. The role focuses on driving continuous improvement, reducing defects, supporting customer satisfaction, and ensuring compliance with IATF 16949, customer-specific requirements, and applicable statutory standards.

The Quality Engineer will act as the primary quality contact for manufacturing operations and will support problem-solving activities, customer concerns, audits, launch activities, and process improvements.

Key Responsibilities /

Functional skills / Hard Skills

Customer Quality

▪ Lead investigation and containment activities for customer complaints and warranty issues.

▪ Coordinate customer concern responses using 8D methodology.

▪ Manage customer portals and ensure timely submission of corrective actions.

▪ Support customer visits, audits, and quality reviews.

▪ Monitor customer quality performance indicators and develop improvement plans.

Process Quality

▪ Ensure manufacturing processes comply with Control Plans, PFMEAs, and customer requirements.

▪ Conduct process audits and layered process audits.

▪ Monitor process capability and quality performance data.

▪ Support production teams in reducing scrap, rework, and defects.

▪ Verify effectiveness of corrective and preventive actions.

Problem Solving & Continual Improvement

▪ Facilitate root cause investigations using:

Pareto Analysis

quality.

▪ Participate in design and process reviews.

Measurement Systems & Data Analysis
