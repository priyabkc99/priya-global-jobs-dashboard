Description

The mobility industry is undergoing unprecedented change.

Astemo is committed to strengthening its business and delivering technological innovation through a strategic business portfolio, which includes Powertrain & Safety Systems, Chassis, Motorcycle, Software and Aftermarket businesses with a collaborative culture and a strong commitment to values-driven leadership.

We are seeking a Quality Engineer to strengthen our Bolton production plant.

This is an excellent opportunity for someone who thrives in cross-functional environments and wants to contribute to meaningful, globally relevant projects.

Your Core Responsibilities Include


Primary interface to Customer on all Quality Issues.
Control of Line and Warranty Returns in accordance with Customer requirements, ensuring adequate countermeasures are taken to prevent reoccurrence.
Monitor and report Quality KPI measures for their relevant section and use to drive improvements in QA performance.
Supporting QA Technicians with product investigations and use of relevant equipment.
Generation of detailed Quality and Test Stand Work Instructions, and support the Technicians in using them.
Control of External and Internal Concession requests, in conjunction with Lead Quality Engineer.
Conduct audits and manage Supplier quality issues as required.
Completion of all relevant documentation in support of Customer QA requirements (ANPQP/APQP/PPAP).
Analysis of process capability data /R&R study data, and working with Production Engineering to improve the production line accordingly.
Understand and deploy Customer Specific Requirements and Astemo standards for their products and ensure continual compliance.


Profile And Qualifications


Strong knowledge of automotive quality standards, particularly IATF 16949, and their practical application.
Proficient in APQP methodologies and core quality tools, including Control Plans, Process Flow, FMEA, SPC, and MSA (R&R).
Experienced in internal auditing and ensuring compliance with automotive quality requirements.
Skilled in structured problem-solving techniques to identify root causes and drive sustainable improvements.
HNC / HND Electrical / Mechanical Engineering as a minimum.
PC Literate(Word/Excel)
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
5 years’ experience in a similar role, ideally gained within automotive. We would consider an electronics, high volume manufacturing assembly background.
Liaison with customers.


Salary : £40-50,000 gross annually

Your future workplace: Astemo Bolton


Production of Air Flow meters, Engine Control Units and Advanced Drivers Assistance Systems and xEV inverters
+100 employees
Benefits: Employer Pension, Private Medical Insurance, Sports and Social Fund, Flexitime, Early finish Friday, Yearly Bonus Scheme, Flexible Holidays, Life Insurance: x4 salary, 25 days holidays with additional length of service days, Electric Vehicle Scheme.
