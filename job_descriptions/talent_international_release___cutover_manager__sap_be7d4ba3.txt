Our client is seeking an experienced SAP & Salesforce Release & Cutover Manager to lead the planning, coordination and execution of releases and cutover activities across a portfolio of technology projects.

This is a hands-on delivery role requiring someone who can manage complex release schedules, cutover plans and cross-project dependencies while coordinating business and technology teams through successful implementation and go-live.
Key Responsibilities

Own end-to-end release and cutover planning across SAP and Salesforce projects
Develop release schedules, cutover plans, runbooks and readiness criteria
Coordinate project, functional, technical, testing, data and business teams
Manage cross-project dependencies, risks, issues and critical path activities
Lead cutover rehearsals, readiness assessments and go/no-go checkpoints
Coordinate data migration, integrations, deployments and business readiness
Provide clear release and cutover reporting to project and senior stakeholders
Coordinate go-live, hypercare and transition into BAU

Key Requirements

Strong experience as a Release Manager, Cutover Manager or Release & Cutover Manager
Demonstrated experience across SAP and/or Salesforce projects
Experience managing releases and cutovers across multiple concurrent technology projects
Strong planning, scheduling, dependency and risk management capability
Experience coordinating technical, functional, testing, data and business teams
Strong understanding of deployment, data migration, integration and go-live activities
Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills
Hands-on, organised approach with the ability to drive delivery

Highly Regarded

Experience across both SAP and Salesforce
Large-scale transformation, implementation or system migration experience
Complex data and integration environments
Utilities, energy or other large enterprise experience

Benefits

Work across a broad portfolio of SAP and Salesforce projects
Take ownership of critical release, cutover and go-live activities
Work within a large, complex enterprise technology environment
