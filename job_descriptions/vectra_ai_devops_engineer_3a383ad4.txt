Position Overview

DevOps Engineers are critical when it comes to keeping both our customers and engineering team running. As part of the DevOps group you will work on products critical to our operations and software that proactively detects issues with systems in the data center, in the cloud, or at customer sites.

Responsibilities

DevOps Engineers will have a variety of responsibilities which may include things like:


Design and implement pipelines and tools to ensure continuous integration, delivery, and deployment for our products
Define and implement tools that enable other teams to follow DevOps best practices
Own and deliver a suite of platform services consumed by internal stakeholders across teams and functions
Work on cloud environments ensuring reliability and availability
Ensure production deployments are fast, reliable, and safe
Work with geo-distributed development teams to improve quality and time-to deploy
Automate, Automate, Automate


Qualifications


Required
Relevant Bachelor's degree (Computer Science, Engineering, etc.)
1-2 years hands-on DevOps/SRE experience
Belief in DevOps and Agile principles
Zealous about automation
Comfort in dealing with Cloud deployments, networking, and security best practices
CI/CD pipeline experience (GitHub Actions, Jenkins, etc.)
Hands on experience on Amazon Web Services (AWS)
Hands on experience with Kubernetes
Linux system proficiency
Preferred
Strong container/serverless experience
Proficient knowledge of Python language
Experience on Hashicorp ecosystem - Terraform, Packer, Consul, Vault
Experience with telemetry tools/pipelines (metrics, logs, and traces)
Experience with RDS databases
