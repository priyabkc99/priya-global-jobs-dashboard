Technical Author | South Wales | Hybrid | Permanent




We’re working with a leading organisation operating within a highly technical and business-critical environment, as they continue to expand their Technical Operations function. They’re looking for an experienced Technical Author to take ownership of creating, improving and maintaining technical and operational documentation across a broad IT estate.




This is a great opportunity for someone who enjoys getting into the detail, working directly with technical teams and turning complex information into documentation that is genuinely useful to the people relying on it.




You’ll work across a varied technology environment including Azure, datacentres, end-user computing and on-premises systems, working closely with technical specialists, subject matter experts and senior stakeholders.




The role

You’ll be responsible for creating and maintaining high-quality technical documentation across a range of IT services and environments.




This will include:

Producing technical documentation, system guides, processes, specifications and operational documentation.
Working directly with technical teams and subject matter experts to understand complex systems and processes.
Translating technical information into clear, concise and accessible content for different audiences.
Establishing and maintaining documentation standards, style guidelines and best practice.
Reviewing and improving existing documentation using feedback, support tickets and stakeholder interviews.
Managing documentation through version control and ensuring content remains aligned with system and software changes.
Reviewing, editing and proofreading documentation to maintain a consistently high standard.
Identifying opportunities to improve the way technical documentation is created, managed and accessed.
Working with stakeholders across technical teams, operations, senior management and end users.




What we’re looking for

We’re looking for someone with proven experience as a Technical Author, Technical Writer or in a similar technical documentation role.




You’ll need to be comfortable understanding technical concepts and working closely with engineers and technical specialists to extract the information needed to produce accurate documentation.




Experience across some of the following would be highly relevant:

Cloud and on-premises environments
Azure
IT infrastructure and operational environments
SaaS and client-server applications
Technical and user-focused documentation
Confluence
Markdown
Git / GitHub
Azure DevOps
Microsoft 365
SharePoint Online




You’ll also need to be a strong communicator who is comfortable working with people at different levels of an organisation, from technical specialists through to senior stakeholders.




Most importantly, you’ll be someone who can work independently, ask the right questions and spot where documentation or processes could be improved.




Why this opportunity?

This is a newly expanding Technical Operations function, so you’ll have the opportunity to make a genuine impact on how technical knowledge is documented and shared across the organisation. You won’t simply be maintaining an existing library of documents. You’ll have the opportunity to help shape documentation standards, improve existing processes and work across a broad and interesting technology environment.




If you’re a technically minded Technical Author who enjoys understanding how things work and finding a simple way to explain them, I’d be keen to hear from you.




Apply now or get in touch with Dan at Yolk Recruitment for a confidential conversation.
