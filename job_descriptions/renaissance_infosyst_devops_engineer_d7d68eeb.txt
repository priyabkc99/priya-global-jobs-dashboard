Role Summary


We are seeking an experienced DevOps Engineer to enable reliable, automated, and secure delivery of enterprise applications and platforms.

Key Responsibilities

Build and manage CI/CD pipelines
Support blue‑green, active‑active, and resilient deployment patterns
Automate environment provisioning and application releases
Monitor system health, performance, and availability
Collaborate with development and platform engineering teams
Skills and Experience

Expert DevSecOps Engineer with hands-on expertise in developing application-side CI/CD pipelines in Azure DevOps using YAML with automation experience.
Possess a minimum of two years' experience authoring Application CI/CD pipelines in YAML and 5+ years overall.
Experienced with DevSecOps tool-chain including Azure DevOps, JFrog, Sonar tooling. Additional experience with DotNet (Containerised apps, SPA, Azure apps) and Java (Maven) applications will be considered advantageous.
Expertise in CI/CD tools and monitoring platforms
Experience supporting mission‑critical systems
Personal Attributes

Strong analytical, diagnostic, and problem-solving capability.
Clear communication and effective stakeholder management.
Ability to work independently and collaboratively across cross-functional teams.
Commitment to operational excellence, automation, knowledge sharing, and continuous improvement.
