Roles and Responsibilities

Project Planning & End-to-End Delivery

Lead the end-to-end planning and delivery of complex DevOps initiatives, from mobilization and scope definition through implementation, rollout, stabilization, and handoff.
Create and maintain an integrated delivery plan covering:
Squad deliverables
Cross-team dependencies
Milestones and timelines
Environment readiness
Testing and approvals
Communications
Rollout activities
Operational readiness
Identify and manage the critical path, ensuring dependencies and deliverables remain aligned with overall project objectives.

Cross-Functional Coordination

Coordinate delivery across one or more DevOps squads and enterprise partner teams, including:
Infrastructure
Cloud
Security
ServiceNow
Application teams
Data teams
Platform teams
Quality Engineering
Production Support
Work closely with Product Owners, Technical Leads, Architects, Engineers, Quality Engineers, Security teams, and business/technology stakeholders to clarify:
Business and technical outcomes
Roles and responsibilities
Delivery sequencing
Acceptance criteria
Dependencies and constraints

Governance & Delivery Management

Establish appropriate governance structures, meeting cadences, decision-making paths, delivery controls, and escalation mechanisms.
Manage and maintain:
Scope
Schedule
Resources
Risks
Issues
Assumptions
Dependencies
Decisions
Changes
Ensure ownership, accountability, and target dates remain clearly defined.
Drive action closure and proactively address delivery gaps, conflicting priorities, resource constraints, access issues, environment blockers, data-quality concerns, and integration dependencies.

Testing, Release & Operational Readiness

Coordinate test planning, user acceptance testing, production-readiness reviews, implementation activities, and post-implementation validation.
Manage required evidence, approvals, and sign-offs for releases and implementations.
Coordinate releases across Development, Stage, and Production environments.
Ensure operational ownership, support processes, documentation, and handoff activities are completed successfully.

Stakeholder Management & Communication

Prepare concise, decision-focused status reports for program and DevOps leadership covering:
Accomplishments
Upcoming milestones
Delivery forecast
Dependencies
Risks and issues
Decisions required
Recovery actions
Coordinate stakeholder communications, readiness activities, demonstrations, information sessions, support planning, and adoption activities.
Translate complex technical delivery details into clear business impacts, risks, and decision points.

Documentation & Continuous Improvement

Ensure deliverables, processes, decisions, and operational ownership are properly documented and transitioned to the appropriate teams.
Apply lessons learned and continuous-improvement practices to:
Improve delivery predictability
Reduce manual effort
Strengthen delivery processes
Reduce recurring operational risks

What You Need to Succeed

Project & Delivery Management

Strong project-management experience delivering complex technology initiatives involving multiple squads, platforms, and enterprise partner teams.
Demonstrated experience working on initiatives involving:
DevOps
Software delivery
Developer platforms
CI/CD
Security controls
Source-code management
Engineering enablement
Ability to build integrated plans, identify the true critical path, manage interdependent deliverables, and hold multiple teams accountable for commitments.

DevOps & Software Delivery Knowledge

Practical understanding of the software development and delivery lifecycle.
Understanding of the relationship between:
Source-code repositories
Build pipelines
Security scanning
Deployment orchestration
Environments
Approvals
Production support
Ability to work effectively with both technical specialists and business stakeholders.

Risk, Issue & Dependency Management

Excellent risk, issue, dependency, and change-management skills.
Sound judgment regarding when to:
Resolve issues independently
Escalate risks
Engage stakeholders
Seek leadership decisions
Strong ability to proactively identify blockers and drive them to resolution.

Leadership & Communication

Excellent facilitation, written communication, organization, and follow-through.
Confidence working with senior leaders, technical specialists, and cross-functional teams.
Ability to operate effectively in a matrixed environment where delivery depends on teams that may not report directly to the Project Manager.
Self-directed, pragmatic, and outcome-focused.
Comfortable bringing structure and clarity to ambiguous or evolving initiatives.




Required Experience

DevOps Tools & Platforms

Experience with standard DevOps tools, practices, and Enterprise Pipeline environments is strongly preferred.
Working knowledge of tools and platforms such as:
Bitbucket / GitHub
Jenkins
CDD
Jira
Confluence
ServiceNow
Checkmarx One
Snyk
Kosli
Grafana
Tableau
Cloud platforms
Infrastructure and deployment technologies

Security & Compliance

Experience coordinating security-control implementation and related delivery activities.
Experience with:
Vulnerability-management workflows
Security exceptions
Compliance evidence
Release gating
Production-readiness activities
Security approvals and controls

Data & Application Management

Experience working with application inventories and application portfolio identifiers.
Familiarity with:
Repository metadata
Data reconciliation
Database updates
Dashboards
ETL processes
Data-quality remediation

Release & Environment Management

Experience supporting releases across Dev, Stage, and Production environments.
Experience coordinating:
Technical testing
Business validation
Implementation
Production deployment
Post-implementation verification
Experience managing dependencies and readiness across multiple technical teams.

Project Management & Agile Tools

Experience with Jira and/or Planview AgilePlace.
Project-management certification or formal Agile delivery training is an asset; however, demonstrated delivery experience is more important than certification alone.
