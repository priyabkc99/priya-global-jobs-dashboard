What are your contributions to the team?


Research, plan, write, and QC technical, maintenance or operational data using engineering and approved data from multiple sources, while ensuring adherence to schedule.
Identify and implement process and product improvements in line with the strategic plan.
Plan, perform and document procedural validations per departmental standards/guidelines, recommending and implementing best use of resources.
Ensure customer and operator requests and issues are analyzed and satisfactorily responded to the originator.
Coordinate and define publications book plan
Define standards and requirements for creation, and for distribution of documentation in an electronic format specification (e.g., S1000D, ATA etc…)
Identify, evaluate, and define action plans and schedules to minimize risks to completion of the publications.



How to thrive in this role?


You hold a college diploma (DEC) in aerospace or equivalent field or a minimum of three (3) years relevant experience in Technical Publications.
You have background in aircraft maintenance, flight crew or as a pilot (asset).
You have strong communication skills (written and spoken).
You are proficient with Microsoft office suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and SAP (an asset.
You have a detailed understanding of Technical Publications processes and technologies (an asset).
You have basic understanding of S1000D and ATA specifications (an asset).
You have good knowledge of HTML, SGML and XML authoring languages (an asset).
You have experience in product development (an asset).
You have experience in defining a troubleshooting methodology in a high technology environment.
You have knowledge and experience in any Bombardier Customer Services and Support (an asset).
You have a good understanding of design procedures and Bombardier Aerospace Engineering System (an asset).
