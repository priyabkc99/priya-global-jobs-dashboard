Job Description

Job Summary

This role could be based in Singapore or India. When you start the application process you will be presented with a drop down menu showing all countries, please ensure that you select a country where the role is based.


The role exists to design, automate, and support DevOps and cloud engineering capabilities that improve release speed, reliability, and control.
It enables secure delivery across CI/CD, infrastructure-as-code, container, security, and observability tooling in regulated banking environments.
It supports teams working across AWS, Azure, Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform, Ansible, ADO, Artifactory, and related platforms.
It requires knowledge of Databricks, Lakehouse concepts, Delta-based data processing, and cloud-based data/ML pipeline orchestration across development, staging, and production environments.


Key Responsibilities


Understanding and knowledge on Implementing automated testing platforms, unit tests, and CICD
Understanding and knowledge on code versioning tools, such as Git, Bitbucket, Azure devops
Understanding and knowledge on Security tools Sonarqube, Aqua, Nexus lifecycle.
Understanding of container platform, such as Docker and Kubernetes
Familiar and working experience with Ansible, Jenkins, Terraform, ADO
Understanding and experience in working on Relation and non-Relation DBs (Postgres, Oracle, MongoDB)
An in depth understanding of Public/Private/Hybrid Cloud solutions and experienced in integrating public cloud into traditional hosting/delivery models with a specific focus on AWS
Understanding and experience on developing cloud native microservices using frameworks such as Python, Nodejs
Understanding and knowledge of Databricks, Delta Lake/Lakehouse patterns, and cloud-based data pipelines for scalable data engineering and MLOps workloads.
Build and maintain insights through Data Analytics applications.
Established technology infrastructure career in engineering and delivery with relevant experience
Strong development background and understanding of how applications are built
AWS Hands-on experience – certified (Preferred)
Experience on both public and private cloud platforms
Experience using containers like docker and Kubernetes
Experience of infrastructure as code using Terraform or similar
Experience in Ansible or similar
Experience on Splunk, ELK stack
Exposure to automate build and deployment pipelines
Banking experience, it’s important the candidate has been exposed to the security and compliance restrictions which banks have to deal with or at least worked in an environment which is regulated
Azure cloud experience is an advantage
Experience with rule and advanced logic creation within SPLUNK is an advantage
A solid understanding of DevOps and Infrastructure as code, must be hands on with DevOps tools and can automate deployments to AWS
Use of DevOps tools to build infrastructure and manage configurations
Databricks Workflows, MLflow and security based on Unity Catalog.
Understanding of 3 tier and microservice architecture. Programming skills in, modern language like Java/Golang/Python
Background in various disciplines is essential, such as Development, Testing, Support, and Engineering. Having a development background with infrastructure experience is an advantage
Significant knowledge of cloud technologies for design and build, both public and private.
A solid understanding of core infrastructure components: OS, network, storage, Database, Web
Knowledge of Virtualised environments required including docker/containerization
Solving complex automation and platform engineering problems.
Building reliable, secure, and scalable delivery platforms.
Working in collaborative teams that operate in regulated, high-control environments.


Our Ideal Candidate


6 +years’ experience in DevOps, Site Reliability Engineering, or related infrastructure/operations role
Strong hands-on experience with CI/CD tooling (e.g. Jenkins, GitLab CI, Azure DevOps, CircleCI) and build/release automation
Deep understanding of cloud platforms (e.g. AWS, Azure, GCP) and containerization/orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes)
Proven ability to collaborate, influence and constructively challenge senior stakeholders (engineering leads, architects, product owners) with clear, logical recommendations
Proactive approach to identifying platform and pipeline issues, anticipating risks, and developing robust, scalable solutions with strong engineering judgment and strategic thinking
Understanding of enterprise technology environments; capacity and curiosity to learn complex banking systems, regulatory constraints, and security requirements
Exceptional verbal and written communication skills, including the ability to produce clear technical documentation and stakeholder presentations (e.g. in PowerPoint, Excel/Sheets for reporting and dashboards)
Bachelor’s degree (or equivalent) in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related technical field


Role Specific Technical Competencies


CI/CD pipelines & DevOps toolchain
Cloud infrastructure (IaaS/PaaS), containers & orchestration
Infrastructure as Code (e.g. Terraform, Ansible, CloudFormation)
Monitoring, logging & observability (e.g. Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, Splunk)
Security, compliance & governance in DevOps (DevSecOps practices)
Scripting & automation (e.g. Python, Bash, PowerShell)
MLOPS,AIOPS automation and practices
Performance, reliability & incident management (SRE practices)
