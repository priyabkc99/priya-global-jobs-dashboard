DevOps Engineer

Up to £120,000

London (4-5 days a week in office)

This is an opportunity for a DevOps Engineer to work at the intersection of infrastructure engineering and AI technology within a high-performance environment. You will play a key role in building and scaling modern infrastructure platforms, with a particular focus on Kubernetes and on-premise environments that support business-critical workloads.

THE COMPANY

They are a globally operating investment and technology-driven organisation with a strong engineering culture. Their teams work closely with technical and business stakeholders to deliver robust, scalable infrastructure across a complex environment.

This role offers exposure to cutting-edge AI infrastructure initiatives while remaining firmly rooted in infrastructure and platform engineering. You will join a collaborative team that values technical excellence, automation, and continuous improvement.

THE ROLE

As a DevOps Engineer you will be responsible for designing, building, and supporting infrastructure and deployment platforms across predominantly on-premise environments.

Specifically, you can expect to be involved in the following:

Building and supporting Kubernetes platforms and containerised workloads
Developing and maintaining CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins and GitHub Actions
Automating infrastructure through Infrastructure as Code using Puppet or Terraform
Managing Linux environments, including security, patching, performance, and systems administration
Creating automation solutions using Bash, Python, or PowerShell
Supporting software delivery, release management, and source control processes
Working closely with infrastructure, platform engineering, and business teams to deliver scalable solutions
Contributing to projects involving AI and high-performance infrastructure environments

SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE

The successful DevOps Engineer will have the following skills and experience:

Strong commercial experience within DevOps, Platform Engineering, Infrastructure Engineering, or Site Reliability Engineering
Hands-on Kubernetes experience, including deployment and administration
Proven experience working within on-premise infrastructure environments
Strong Linux administration and troubleshooting skills
Experience with Infrastructure as Code tools such as Puppet or Terraform
CI/CD experience using Jenkins, GitHub Actions, or similar technologies
Scripting experience with Bash, Python, or PowerShell
Experience using Git-based source control tools
Strong communication skills and the ability to work with both technical and non-technical stakeholders
Experience working within Financial Services, Trading Technology or other regulated environments

BENEFITS

The successful Dev Ops Engineer will receive the following benefits:

Salary up to £120,000 - depending on experience
Up to 50% bonus

HOW TO APPLY
