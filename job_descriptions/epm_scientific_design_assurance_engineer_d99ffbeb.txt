Overview



Our client is seeking an experienced Design Assurance Engineer to support a strategic product transfer programme within a regulated medical device environment. This individual will serve as the quality representative between R&D and Manufacturing, ensuring product design intent, risk controls, and regulatory requirements are maintained throughout the transfer process.

The successful candidate will work closely with R&D, Quality, Manufacturing Engineering, Regulatory Affairs, and Operations teams to ensure the seamless transfer of products from development into commercial manufacturing.







Key Responsibilities


Lead Design Assurance activities supporting product transfer and product lifecycle projects.
Act as a key liaison between R&D and Manufacturing to ensure successful transfer of products and processes.
Partner with R&D teams to understand design intent, critical product requirements, and risk controls.
Drive Risk Management activities in accordance with ISO 14971.
Develop, review, and maintain Risk Management Files, Hazard Analyses, dFMEAs and pFMEAs.
Assess the impact of design, process, material, supplier, and manufacturing changes on product performance and patient safety.
Support design reviews and provide quality and risk-based guidance to project teams.
Ensure Design Control requirements are maintained throughout development and transfer activities.
Collaborate with R&D, Operations, Manufacturing Engineering, Quality, Supply Chain, and Regulatory Affairs functions to resolve technical challenges.
Support design verification, validation, and transfer-related activities as required.
Review and approve validation documentation, technical reports, and change controls.
Participate in CAPA investigations, root cause analysis, and continuous improvement activities.
Communicate project risks, issues, and mitigation plans effectively across cross-functional teams.
