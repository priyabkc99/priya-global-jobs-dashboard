About our team:

Radiant is redefining how AI infrastructure is built.

We design and operate AI-native infrastructure platforms engineered for sovereignty, performance, and scale — powering GPU-native workloads, multi-tenant control planes, and high-performance AI systems for the most demanding environments. We are building purpose-built AI infrastructure from powered land, to compute, to software.

As we scale our operations and deploy capital into the next generation of AI infrastructure, we are looking to expand our finance team with leaders who can combine technical strength with execution excellence and are driven to build.

Radiant was established by Brookfield, a leading global alternative asset manager with over US$1 trillion of assets under management across real estate, infrastructure, renewable power and transition, private equity and credit. Brookfield's global relationships, investment expertise and access to long-term institutional capital provide Radiant with a differentiated platform from which to develop, finance and operate AI infrastructure assets. This combination of entrepreneurial execution and institutional sponsorship enables Radiant to pursue large-scale GPU and AI infrastructure opportunities globally.

Job Summary:

As a Senior Technical Writer, you will own and improve the technical documentation experience across Radiant’s platform, helping developers, customers, and internal teams understand, adopt, and operate our products effectively.

You’ll work closely with Product, Engineering, SRE, Support, and Solutions teams to turn complex technical concepts into clear, accurate, practical documentation across APIs, infrastructure, platform services, integrations, and operational workflows.

This is a hands-on role for someone who is comfortable getting close to the technology, testing.

Key Responsibilities:


Own and evolve Radiant’s developer and product documentation, including guides, tutorials, API documentation, reference material, troubleshooting guides, and operational content.
Work closely with Product and Engineering to ensure documentation is included as part of the product development and release process.
Translate complex technical concepts into clear, concise, and accessible content for different technical audiences.
Create and maintain getting-started guides, how-to content, examples, architecture explanations, and best-practice documentation.
Test documented workflows directly to ensure content is accurate, complete, and usable.
Improve the structure, navigation, discoverability, and consistency of Radiant’s documentation.
Partner with Support and Solutions teams to identify common customer issues and turn them into better documentation and self-service content.
Establish documentation standards, templates, style guidelines, and processes that help engineering teams contribute effectively.
Track documentation quality and usage, using feedback and product data to continuously improve the experience.


Qualifications:


Experience writing documentation for technical products, cloud platforms, developer tools, infrastructure, APIs, or SaaS products.
Strong technical fluency and the ability to understand concepts across:
Cloud & infrastructure: Kubernetes, containers, networking, compute, storage
APIs & integrations: REST APIs, authentication, webhooks, SDKs
Developer workflows: CLI tools, Git, CI/CD, configuration and automation
Observability & operations: logging, metrics, monitoring and troubleshooting
Comfortable reading code, API specifications, configuration files, architecture diagrams, and technical designs.
Experience with docs-as-code workflows, Markdown, Git, and documentation tooling such as Docusaurus, MkDocs, GitBook, ReadMe, or similar.
Able to work directly with engineers and subject matter experts to extract the information needed without relying on fully formed source material.
Excellent written communication, editing, information architecture, and attention to detail.
Comfortable operating in a fast-paced environment where products and documentation evolve quickly.


What you bring:


Systems thinking: Understand how infrastructure, services, data, and operational workflows fit together.
Product thinking: Treat internal teams as customers and focus on measurable outcomes.
Technical fluency: Able to work credibly across APIs, integrations, infrastructure, data, and architecture.
Prioritisation: Balance operational pain, strategic value, technical complexity, and business impact.
Ownership: Drive ambiguous problems from discovery and definition through to adoption and iteration.
