The Role

The DevOps Engineer plays a key role in designing, building, and operating APM’s cloud and platform capabilities, enabling secure, reliable, and scalable technology delivery across the organisation.

This role is responsible for driving automation, standardisation, and DevSecOps practices across the technology lifecycle, supporting both infrastructure and application delivery through modern engineering approaches.

Based in Perth, Sydney, or Melbourne, this is an exciting opportunity to work within a global environment and contribute to the evolution of APM’s cloud platforms and engineering capability.

In This Role You Will


Design, build, and operate Azure cloud environments across development, test, and production
Develop and maintain CI/CD pipelines using Azure DevOps and/or GitHub Actions
Implement and manage Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using Terraform
Embed DevSecOps practices, including security controls, scanning, and governance in pipelines
Automate infrastructure and application delivery to improve reliability and reduce manual effort
Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting using Azure-native tools
Support incident response, problem management, and root cause analysis
Collaborate with engineering, infrastructure, and security teams to improve platform maturity
Contribute to platform standards, architecture, and roadmaps
Drive continuous improvement through automation, standardisation, and optimisation initiatives
Participate in an on-call support rotation to support critical services


Skills You’ll Bring


5+ years’ experience working in Azure / Microsoft cloud environments or Systems Engineering roles
Strong hands-on experience with Terraform and Infrastructure as Code principles
Proven experience building and managing CI/CD pipelines (Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions)
Strong understanding of cloud architecture, networking, identity, and security
Experience implementing DevSecOps practices, including security scanning and governance
Knowledge of monitoring and observability tools (e.g. Azure Monitor, Log Analytics, App Insights)
Familiarity with security frameworks and standards (e.g. NIST, ISO, CIS)
Exposure to cloud security, vulnerability management, and compliance requirements
Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to work in a fast-paced, evolving environment
Excellent communication and collaboration skills across technical and business stakeholders


What’s in it for you?

Working with a global organisation, you’ll play a key role in shaping and evolving APM’s cloud and platform engineering capabilities.

You’ll Have The Opportunity To


Work with modern technologies and contribute to next-generation cloud platforms
Embed best-practice DevOps and DevSecOps frameworks across a growing organisation
Influence how applications and infrastructure are delivered at scale
Grow your career within a global business spanning 11 countries


We offer flexible / hybrid working, access to the latest tools and technology, additional leave options, health discounts, and a comprehensive range of employee benefits.

About APM Group

We’re a diverse team of over 16,000 people across 11 countries with a shared purpose - to enable better lives. Our purpose is the common thread that lives in everything we do, and it starts with enabling better lives for our employees and their families too.

Our work enables the people we serve to realise their ambitions and aspirations through sustainable employment, independence, better health, and wellbeing, and increased social participation.

When you join APM, you’ll have the opportunity to grow your career across global brands and geographies while making a meaningful difference.

At APM we are strengthened by diversity. We are committed to creating a workplace where everyone feels included, respected, and valued.
