My client, a global trading firm, is actively seeking a Senior DevOps Engineer to join their team in Hong Kong.




The role:

Play a pivotal role in modernizing the deployment system by evaluating and selecting new technologies, engaging developers, collaborating with DevOps and trading system teams, and overseeing the migration process.
Seamlessly integrate the new deployment system into the development workflow, focusing on automating deployment and testing processes.
Automate developer tools, streamline workflows, and facilitate trader script deployment to enhance operational efficiency.
Create real-time monitoring solutions to optimize the performance of IT teams company-wide.
Engineer and enhance data pipelines to efficiently deliver both real-time and historical data to Quant and Trading teams.
Offer technical leadership and mentorship to junior team members.




What you offer:

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field
Over 5 years of professional experience in roles such as DevOps, Systems Engineering, or similar
Proficiency in modern deployment systems, orchestration tools, and CI/CD pipelines
Demonstrated ability to drive organizational changes, coordinate activities across teams
Skill in managing conflicting requirements from various stakeholders
Strong programming expertise in Python or Golang, and shell scripting
Hands-on experience in Linux system administration
Familiarity with Kafka
Excellent English communication skills, both written and verbal
Collaborative mindset for working with other teams to achieve shared goals
Emphasis on automation and adherence to standard practices
Confident individual with exceptional interpersonal abilities
Comfortable thriving in a fast-paced work environment




The sell:

Competitive base salary with a potential bonus of up to 6 months
Flat structure
Fully stacked pantry and pool table!
