Client Delivery Director at Be-IT | Helping Organisations Build High-Performing Technology Teams | Permanent & Contract Recruitment

We're working with a highly successful technology-driven organisation seeking a Senior Platform & DevOps Engineer to join a small, high-impact engineering team responsible for building and evolving cloud platforms that power critical applications.




This isn't a traditional "keep the lights on" infrastructure role.

You'll join a collaborative team of seven engineers where platform engineering, DevOps, automation and cloud infrastructure come together. The environment is built around innovation, ownership and engineering excellence, giving you the freedom to challenge existing approaches, introduce new ideas and directly influence technical direction.

The team already has a handful of engineers who thrive on autonomy and enjoy solving complex problems through automation, modern platform engineering practices and continuous improvement. They're looking for a third engineer with a similar mindset who wants to help shape what's next.




This role would suit someone who is already a strong engineer but still wants to learn, grow and broaden their expertise across platform engineering, DevOps and Infrastructure as Code.




What You'll Be Doing

Designing, building and operating cloud infrastructure in Microsoft Azure
Developing and improving CI/CD capabilities and deployment automation
Working extensively with Kubernetes and container-based platforms
Building reusable Infrastructure as Code solutions using Terraform
Driving platform standardisation, automation and engineering best practice
Improving observability, monitoring and operational excellence
Collaborating closely with software engineers, architects and technology stakeholders
Evaluating and introducing new tools, technologies and approaches where they add value
Taking ownership of complex technical challenges from design through to delivery




We're keen to speak with engineers who combine both Platform Engineering and DevOps experience.

A lot of applicants have strong platform backgrounds but limited DevOps exposure. For this role, both areas are important.




You'll ideally have experience with:

Microsoft Azure
Kubernetes (essential)
Terraform
GitLab, GitHub Actions, Azure DevOps or similar CI/CD tooling
Infrastructure as Code
Docker and containerisation
GitOps concepts and tooling such as ArgoCD
Observability tooling such as Grafana, Prometheus, Splunk or similar
Bash, PowerShell, Python or Go
Agile delivery environments




The Kind of Engineer Who Will Thrive Here

You'll likely enjoy this role if you:

Love automating repetitive processes
Like building platforms rather than simply administering them
Enjoy introducing new ideas and technologies
Take ownership of problems and see them through to completion
Are comfortable rolling up your sleeves and remaining hands-on
Want to work in a small team where your contributions genuinely influence technical decisions




Interview Talking Points

Successful candidates will typically be comfortable discussing topics such as:

Designing reusable CI/CD components and templates
Kubernetes operations and GitOps practices
ArgoCD and deployment lifecycle management
Metrics, logs and distributed tracing
Terraform workflows and Infrastructure as Code principles
Container security and vulnerability management
Platform reliability, scalability and automation




Why Join?

Salary of £80,000 - £110,000
Small, highly collaborative engineering team
Significant autonomy and ownership
Opportunity to influence platform strategy
Strong focus on innovation and modern engineering practices
Hybrid working with 3 days per week in Central London
Excellent opportunity for an ambitious engineer looking to accelerate their growth while remaining hands-on




This is a chance to join a business where platform engineering is viewed as a strategic capability, not simply an operational function, and where your ideas can have a visible impact from day one.
