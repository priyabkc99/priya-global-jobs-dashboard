Agilysis is looking for a Workday Release Manager ( Workday Transformation ).




Roles & Responsibilities:

Understanding of Workday release mechanics: preview tenants, feature activation, backwards compatibility, and Known Issue management
Working knowledge of Workday HCM functional areas: Core HR, Payroll (including integrations), Talent, Absence
Familiarity with Workday integration framework (Studio, EIB, Core Connectors) sufficient to assess integration impact of releases
Ability to read and interpret Workday release notes and translate them into business and technical impact assessments
Experience with Workday Community: navigating release documentation, Known Issues, and Workday Brainstorm

Program & Governance Skills

Strong command of release management frameworks (ITIL, SAFe, or equivalent) and their application in SaaS environments
Experience leading Change Advisory Boards and Go/No-Go governance forums
Proficiency in managing cross-functional, cross-geography dependencies in complex matrix organizations
Structured approach to risk identification, escalation, and mitigation planning

Qualifications & Experience:

10+ years of experience in release management, program delivery, or enterprise IT transformation
Proven experience managing Workday releases through the biannual update cycle (R1/R2), including preview evaluation, impact analysis, and opt-in governance
Experience of at least one large-scale, multi-country Workday HCM implementation (preferred)
Demonstrated track record managing payroll-integrated system releases, with understanding of payroll parallel run sequencing
Familiarity with Workday tenant architecture, environment types (Sandbox, Implementation, Production), and promotion workflows
Bachelor's or Master's degree in Information Technology, Engineering, Business Administration, or related discipline

Location : Mumbai, India (Preferred)
