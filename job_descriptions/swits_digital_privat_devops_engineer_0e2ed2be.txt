Role:Devops Engineer

Location: Chennai

Experience: 6-12

Domain: HealthCare

Responsibilities


Deploy, automate, maintain, and manage systems ensuring high availability, performance, and scalability across a large-scale multi-account AWS environment
Design and implement GitOps-based CI/CD pipelines for continuous delivery across Dev, QA, UAT, and Production environments
Author and maintain Helm charts for Kubernetes application deployments on AWS
Provision and manage cloud infrastructure as code across multiple AWS accounts and regions
Manage secrets and configuration securely integrated with application workloads
Deploy and maintain applications on on-premises Linux servers alongside cloud-based container workloads
Implement and enhance observability pipelines for logging, monitoring, and alerting
Troubleshoot full-stack issues spanning containers, AWS networking, Oracle DB, MongoDB, and application layers
Collaborate with senior engineers and architects on enterprise-level infrastructure initiatives
Proactively review, recommend, and implement infrastructure changes with proper validation across all environments


Qualifications


5+ years of experience in DevOps, SRE, or platform engineering
Strong hands-on experience with AWS container services (EKS or ECS), ECR, S3, CloudFront, IAM, VPC, and Parameter Store
Experience with Kubernetes or container orchestration platforms deployments, services, ingress, RBAC, autoscaling, and secrets management; EKS experience preferred
Experience with GitOps-based CI/CD pipelines using tools such as GitLab CI, Jenkins, or GitHub Actions; ArgoCD or Flux experience is a plus
Experience authoring and maintaining Helm charts for multi-environment deployments
Hands-on experience with Terraform for infrastructure provisioning; Terragrunt experience is a plus
Experience with Docker building, optimizing, and debugging container images
Familiarity with on-premises Linux server administration (RHEL/Ubuntu, systemd, Ansible)
Experience with observability tooling Splunk, OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, Grafana, or similar
Familiarity with JIRA, Confluence, and collaborative engineering workflows
Experience in HIPAA-regulated or compliance-sensitive environments is a plus
Self-motivated, technically curious, and comfortable working independently
