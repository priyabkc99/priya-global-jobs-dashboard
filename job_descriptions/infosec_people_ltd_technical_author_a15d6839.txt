Senior Technical Author

£53,000 - £6100 + Bonus + Excellent Benefits

Hybrid Working




We're partnering with a leading organisation operating within the UK's critical infrastructure sector to recruit a Senior Technical Author to join a growing Technology Operations team.




This is an opportunity to take ownership of technical documentation standards across a complex enterprise environment spanning cloud platforms, data centres, end-user computing and operational services.




You'll work closely with architects, engineers, cyber security professionals and senior stakeholders to create clear, engaging and accessible documentation that supports operational excellence.




What You'll Be Doing

Producing high-quality technical and operational documentation
Creating user guides, process documentation and technical specifications
Establishing documentation standards, templates and style guides
Managing document lifecycle and version control processes
Collaborating with technical SMEs across infrastructure, cloud and security teams
Improving documentation quality through stakeholder feedback and operational insight
Supporting continuous improvement across IT operations




What We're Looking For

Proven experience as a Technical Author, Technical Writer or Documentation Specialist
Experience documenting enterprise IT systems and services
Ability to translate technical concepts into user-friendly content
Strong stakeholder engagement skills
Experience with Microsoft 365 and SharePoint Online
Experience with Git, GitHub and Azure DevOps
Knowledge of Confluence and Markdown
Degree in IT, Computer Science or a related discipline
