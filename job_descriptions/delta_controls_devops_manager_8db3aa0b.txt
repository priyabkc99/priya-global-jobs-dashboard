About Delta Intelligent Building Technologies (Canada) Inc.(formerly known as Delta Controls).

Delta Intelligent Building Technologies (Canada) Inc. (a subsidiary of Delta Electronics) is a leading building controls manufacturer with over 300 partners in more than 80 countries. For over three decades, we have provided innovative building automation solutions for commercial, healthcare, education, and leisure facilities. We delivered the world’s first fully integrated BACnet building solution for HVAC, lighting, and access control.

Job Summary

The DevOps Manager owns DevOps practice, tooling, and release management across the entire Product Development department. This role is accountable for release and deployment success and works collaboratively with each product's DevOps Specialist and team lead to resolve issues. The role also owns operations and maintenance of the department's shared DevOps tooling, including source control and code quality platforms, and owns the enterprise On-Call/incident response program for SaaS applications.

DevOps Specialists embedded within individual product teams continue to report administratively to their Team Leads or Enterprise Directors; the DevOps Manager is their functional manager, setting technical direction and standards for the discipline org-wide, without holding direct people-management responsibility over them.

Duties & Responsibilities

Responsibilities include but are not limited to:

Release & Pipeline Management


Accountable for the overall success of build and deployment processes across all PD products — reliability, consistency, and adherence to DevOps standards — partnering with each product's DevOps Specialist and team lead to diagnose and resolve issues
Define and promote consistent merge/branching/release standards across PD; work with product teams to adopt them
Advise and coordinate on deployment windows and cadence for products to avoid cross-team conflicts
Maintain and continually improve CI/CD infrastructure standards across PD


Enterprise DevOps Tooling


Own operations, maintenance, and administration of enterprise DevOps tooling used across Product Development (GitLab, SonarQube, Jenkins, and others)
Ensure availability, performance, and security of these platforms, including upgrades, patching, and capacity planning
Manage access, permissions, and integration of these tools across product teams and global offices
Evaluate, select, and onboard new enterprise DevOps tooling as the department's needs evolve
Maintain licensing, vendor relationships, and cost management for owned tooling


Functional Management of DevOps Specialists


Set technical direction, standards, and best practices for all DevOps Specialists in PD
Provide technical mentorship, coaching, and career guidance to DevOps Specialists across the organization
Conduct technical reviews of DevOps Specialists' work as input to their performance reviews (in partnership with their direct managers)
Identify practice or performance concerns and raise them with the DevOps Specialist's manager; work collaboratively with the engineer to implement the agreed resolution
Participate in hiring processes for new DevOps Specialists across PD (technical evaluation/input)


On-Call Program Ownership


Own the enterprise on-call/incident response program in its entirety, including tooling, escalation policies, and rotation structure
Drive the phased rollout: runbook completion, tool launch, and progression to full 24x7 coverage
Set and enforce runbook standards and completion requirements across product teams
Own incident response process design, including severity definitions, escalation paths, and post-incident review process
Coordinate with product/team leads to ensure adequate on-call staffing and rotation fairness


General


Continually improve DevOps and release practices based on incident trends, release outcomes, and team feedback
Report on release health, deployment frequency/failure rate, and on-call/incident metrics to the Chief Product Development Officer


Required Or Preferred Skills And Abilities


Strong technical communication and cross-team influence skills — able to enforce standards across multiple offices and teams
Demonstrated experience managing or leading release/deployment processes at scale, across more than one product line
Experience administering enterprise DevOps tooling (e.g., GitLab, SonarQube, or equivalents)
Experience designing or running an on-call/incident response program
Knowledge of CI/CD tooling and practices (e.g., GitLab CI/CD, Jenkins, Kubernetes/Helm)
Ability to mentor, coach, and hold accountable team members outside a direct reporting line
Outstanding diagnostic and problem-solving skills
Knowledge of SDLC, application architecture, deployment, and infrastructure practices across multiple products
Conflict resolution skills, particularly for cross-team/cross-office release disputes


Minimum Qualifications


College diploma or University degree in computer science, information systems, or software engineering, or equivalent experience
5+ years of experience in DevOps, release engineering, or related infrastructure roles
Prior experience in a functional/matrixed management role (technical authority without direct people-management authority) preferred
Experience running or building an incident response/on-call program preferred
Experience administering source control and code quality platforms at scale preferred
