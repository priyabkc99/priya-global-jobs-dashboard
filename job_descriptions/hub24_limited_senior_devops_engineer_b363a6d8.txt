Why this is an exciting opportunity

The Senior DevOps Engineer is a senior technical specialist responsible for designing, implementing, automating, and continuously improving cloud platforms, infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, observability solutions, and operational excellence practices. This role partners closely with Platform, Cyber Security, Software Engineering, Product, and Test teams to deliver secure, scalable, reliable, and highly available technology solutions. The position provides technical leadership, drives DevOps maturity, and champions automation, innovation, and engineering best practices across the organisation.

What You’ll Be Doing

Cloud & Platform Engineering


Design, implement, and maintain scalable cloud platforms across AWS and/or Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
Develop and manage Infrastructure as Code (IaC) solutions using Terraform, CloudFormation, or similar technologies.
Establish cloud networking, security, identity management, governance, and platform standards.
Build reusable infrastructure modules to support consistency and operational efficiency.
Design, administer, and optimise container platforms, including Kubernetes environments.


DevOps & Automation


Design, implement, and optimise CI/CD pipelines and deployment frameworks.
Automate software build, testing, deployment, and release processes.
Drive adoption of DevOps principles, developer enablement, and self-service platform capabilities.
Establish engineering standards, controls, quality gates, and automation-first practices.


Reliability, Security & Operations


Implement and enhance monitoring, logging, tracing, and alerting capabilities.
Support Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) practices, including SLIs, SLOs, error budgets, resilience testing, and service reliability improvements.
Lead production incident management, root cause analysis, and continuous service improvement initiatives.
Manage vulnerability remediation, patching, secrets management, and compliance requirements.
Collaborate with Cyber Security teams to ensure secure-by-design solutions and adherence to governance standards.


Technical Leadership


Provide technical leadership, mentoring, coaching, and peer reviews across engineering teams.
Contribute to architecture decisions, technology roadmaps, and platform strategy.
Lead the resolution of complex technical challenges and operational issues.
Drive continuous improvement initiatives and promote engineering excellence across the organisation.


What You Bring

You don’t need to tick every box, but experience in the below will set you up for success.

Experience & Skills


Minimum 7 years' experience in DevOps, Cloud Engineering, Platform Engineering, or related disciplines.
Proven experience operating and supporting enterprise-scale cloud environments.
Demonstrated success implementing DevOps, automation, and platform engineering practices.
Experience leading cloud migration, platform transformation, or modernization initiatives.
Experience supporting business-critical production systems in high-availability environments.
Experience working within Agile delivery frameworks.
Relevant certifications such as AWS Solutions Architect, AWS DevOps Engineer, Kubernetes, or Terraform are highly desirable.


Leadership & Communication


Strong stakeholder engagement and relationship management skills.
Ability to influence technical direction and engineering standards.
Proven mentoring and coaching capability.
Excellent verbal, written, documentation, and presentation skills.
Ability to lead technical initiatives across multiple teams and functions.
Takes ownership and accountability for outcomes and commitments.
Demonstrates a strong focus on operational excellence and service reliability.
Continuously seeks opportunities to improve efficiency, automation, and customer outcomes.
Embraces innovation, AI capabilities, and automation-first thinking.
Promotes collaboration, knowledge sharing, and team success.
Prioritises reliability, performance, security, and user experience.
Remains calm and effective when managing incidents and competing priorities.
Adapts quickly to changing business and technology requirements.
Acts with professionalism, transparency, and integrity.
Upholds security, compliance, and governance standards in all activities.
