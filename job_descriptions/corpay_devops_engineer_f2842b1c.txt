What We Need

Corpay is currently looking to hire a DevOps Engineer within our Buyatab division. This position falls under our Gift card line of business and is located in Vancouver, Canada. You will report into the DevOps Team Lead In this role and will join a high-performing DevOps team and collaborate with highly skilled Agile development teams to build and develop scalable and high traffic eCommerce solutions hosted in a cloud platform. Buyatab, a subsidiary of Stored Value Solutions and Corpay, Inc., is not just about team lunches, fun company events or puppies in the office (although we have all 3!). A FinTech maverick and officially one of the most popular technology companies in BC, Buyatab is a leading supplier of advanced online gift card infrastructure, technology and marketing services for leading brands. Working with some of the world’s leading brands (including Four Seasons, Fairmont, Whole Foods Market, Lowes), we are recognized for our solution and design flexibility, focus on client brand standards, high-quality customer support, and fraud protection guarantee. As a result, our clients are positioned to grow their gift card business, enhance their brands, gain a competitive edge, and leverage the rapid growth in mobile device use and social media.

How We Work

As a DevOps Engineer you will be expected to work out of our Vancouver office location. Corpay will set you up for success by providing:


Company-issued equipment
Assigned workspace in our Vancouver office
Formal, hands-on training


Role Responsibilities

The responsibilities of the role will include:


Planning, implementing, and deploying AWS infrastructure changes that improve platform reliability, scalability, security, and deployment velocity
Working closely with software development teams to design, automate, and support AWS-based infrastructure and deployment pipelines
Designing and maintaining secure, scalable, and highly available cloud infrastructure on AWS
Supporting and enhancing CI/CD pipelines to enable frequent, safe, and repeatable deployments
Collaborating with engineering teams to define infrastructure, deployment, and operational requirements early in the development lifecycle
Monitoring and proactively addressing infrastructure issues, escalating and proposing solutions when necessary
Contributing to infrastructure standards, best practices, and documentation
Building strong working relationships across teams and with external partners, communicating infrastructure needs clearly and effectively


Required

Qualifications and Skills:


Degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience
3–5 years of experience working in an Agile software development environment
3–5 years of experience building, operating, and supporting AWS infrastructure
Hands-on experience with AWS services such as VPC, EC2, Lambda, IAM, Route 53, S3, and infrastructure-as-code tools (e.g., CloudFormation or Terraform)
Experience enabling and supporting CI/CD pipelines using tools such as Jenkins, AWS CodePipeline, AWS CodeDeploy, or CircleCI
Practical experience with Git, Docker, and deploying applications to AWS environments
Solid understanding of networking concepts and Linux and/or Windows Server administration
Experience with scripting languages such as PowerShell, Python, or bash
Familiarity with automation and configuration management tools (e.g., PowerShell DSC, Chef, Ansible)
Experience enabling and using metrics, logging, and monitoring within cloud infrastructure


Nice to Have


Familiarity with DevSecOps practices and cloud security controls
Experience with Gerrit Code Review
Exposure to high-traffic or customer-facing production systems


Soft Skills


Strong written and verbal communication skills
Ability to collaborate across teams and explain technical concepts clearly
Demonstrated curiosity, adaptability, and willingness to learn new technologies
A pragmatic DevOps mindset with a focus on continuous improvement


Benefits & Perks

Time-off including major holidays, vacation, sick, personal, & volunteer time
Discounted gym membership rate
Philanthropic support with both local and national organizations
Fun culture with company-wide contests and prizes
