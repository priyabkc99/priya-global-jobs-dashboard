Reference: ITA006273-Jess-1

A leading South African technology and engineering business is looking for a DevOps Engineer to join a high-performing multidisciplinary team developing innovative IoT, energy management, and operational intelligence solutions across Africa.
This is an exciting opportunity for a DevOps professional who enjoys building scalable cloud infrastructure, automating processes, and supporting cutting-edge technology solutions that deliver real-world impact.
Duties & Responsibilities
What You'll Be Doing

Own, design, deploy, and maintain cloud infrastructure and services
Manage and optimise containerised environments for performance and cost efficiency
Build, maintain, and improve CI/CD pipelines
Implement infrastructure security controls, compliance measures, and vulnerability management processes
Develop monitoring, observability, and alerting solutions using modern tooling
Troubleshoot production incidents and implement preventative improvements
Support products and services throughout their full lifecycle, including deployments, enhancements, and releases
Collaborate closely with Software Engineers, QA teams, and Operations stakeholders to streamline delivery and improve reliability
 
Minimum Requirements

Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or equivalent relevant qualification
2 to 5 years of DevOps experience
Strong problem-solving and analytical skills
Excellent communication abilities
Experience writing technical documentation
Practical experience with Linux environments
Experience with scripting using Bash, Python, and/or Node.js
Strong containerisation experience
Experience with automation tools such as Terraform, Ansible, Jenkins, or GitLab CI/CD
Database administration and design experience, preferably PostgreSQL and TimescaleDB
Familiarity with cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, GCP, Hetzner, or similar cloud technologies
Working knowledge of quality management processes and standards
 
Advantageous Experience

UI/UX exposure
AWS DevOps Engineer certification or similar cloud certifications
 
Application Note:
If you have not received feedback within two weeks, please consider your application unsuccessful. Your profile will remain in our database for future opportunities
Package & Remuneration
R 600 000 - R 630 000 - Annually
