Role Overview

We are seeking an experienced Release Manager to drive the end‑to‑end release lifecycle across multiple products and environments. The role requires strong coordination across engineering, QA, DevOps, and product teams, ensuring predictable, high‑quality releases. In addition, the candidate should have worked in driving QA processes and test automation, enabling them to effectively drive release quality and readiness.
