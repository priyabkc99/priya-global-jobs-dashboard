About impact.com:

impact.com is the world's leading commerce partnership marketing platform, transforming the way businesses grow by enabling them to discover, manage, and scale partnerships across the entire customer journey. From affiliates and influencers to content publishers, brand ambassadors, and customer advocates, impact.com empowers brands to drive trusted, performance-based growth through authentic relationships. Its award-winning products - Performance (affiliate), Creator (influencer), and Advocate (customer referral) - unify every type of partner into one integrated platform. As consumers increasingly rely on recommendations from people and communities they trust, impact.com helps brands show up where it matters most. Today, over 5,000 global brands - including Walmart, Uber, Shopify, Lenovo, L'Oreal, and Fanatics - rely on impact.com to power more than 350,000 partnerships that deliver measurable business results.

Your Role at impact.com:

As a DevOps/Cloud Engineer, you will be responsible for working hands-on to design, develop and implement processes that automate the build, staging, release & deployment of the software platform. You will also be responsible for infrastructure provisioning through build pipelines. As an enabler of engineering, you will have a direct impact on the productivity of the systems development life cycle.

What You’ll Do:

The main purpose of the DevOps/Cloud Engineer is to homogenize development and operations, and to create a collaborative and complementary development culture.

The methods through which this is most pronounced is automation; including, but not limited to:


Continuous integration & deployment
Continuous code quality monitoring
Continuous security scanning & license compliance monitoring
Configuration management
Infrastructure as Code
Increase efficiency and optimal use of organizational resources
Increase cross-team collaboration and employee satisfaction
Support engineers in debugging & resolving issues surrounding build and staging & production deployments


What You’ll Bring:


Previous experience as a Cloud / DevOps / Platform Engineer in a Google Cloud (GCP) environment
Automation and Configuration Management
CI/CD tools, preferably experience in Jenkins administration and writing pipelines
Experience with Terraform to programmatically provision the required resources
Experience using building tools
Programming and/or scripting language competency, with at least one of; Shell, Java, Groovy
Gradle with Groovy configuration & plugin authoring, or equivalent Maven experience
Self starter / Adaptability / Drive / Social Skills / Personal Development / Initiative


Nice to Have:


Affiliate & Partnerships Industry Fundamentals Certification by PXA


Salary Range: $110,000 - $140,000 CAD per year, plus an additional 5% variable annual bonus contingent on Company performance with eligibility to receive Restricted Stock Unit (RSU) grant. *This is the pay range the Company believes is equitable for this position at the time of this posting.
