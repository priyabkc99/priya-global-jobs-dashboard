As one of the world´s leading manufacturers of cardio- and endovascular medical devices, BIOTRONIK is headquartered in Berlin, Germany, and represented in over 100 countries by its global workforce. The work of our committed, highly specialized, exceptionally skilled employees results in crucial advancements in the world of cardiology and changes the lives of patients every day.

The DevOps Engineer is reporting to the Director Software Development & Digital Health. He/She is responsible for building and operating the cloud and hybrid infrastructure that supports BIOTRONIK's medical device software, ensuring secure, reliable, and auditable delivery of software to production.

Your Responsibilities


Design, write, and maintain Terraform to provision and manage Azure infrastructure as code, following infrastructure-as-code best practices.
Manage Kubernetes clusters (AKS), Helm chart deployments, ArgoCD GitOps workflows, and associated networking components.
Build and maintain GitHub Actions CI/CD pipelines for automated application delivery, container image promotion, and infrastructure deployment, including automated security and quality gates.
Deploy, operate, and support applications across multi-stage environments, from development through production, using structured release and promotion strategies.
Manage secrets, identity, and access using cloud-native security practices, including Key Vault and role-based access control.
Maintain observability platforms and develop monitoring, logging, and alerting solutions to support proactive incident detection.
Bridge hybrid on-premises and Azure environments through secure networking and tunnel-based connectivity solutions.
Participate in an on-call rotation, support incident response, and drive post-incident improvements to infrastructure and pipelines.
Maintain backup and disaster recovery procedures and support cloud cost and capacity management.
Work closely with regulatory affairs and compliance teams to ensure infrastructure and deployment processes adhere to industry standards and regulations.
Collaborate with software engineers and test engineers to embed infrastructure and pipeline requirements early in the development lifecycle.


Your Profile


Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field; advanced degree preferred.
At least 5-7 years of relevant work experience in DevOps, infrastructure, or platform engineering.
At least 3 years of relevant work experience in the medical device industry, or another regulated/audit-heavy industry (e.g. healthcare, FinTech, aerospace), would be an added advantage.
Proven experience writing and maintaining Terraform for Azure infrastructure.
Experience operating Kubernetes (AKS preferred) in production, including Helm and GitOps tooling such as ArgoCD.
Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines, preferably GitHub Actions.
Familiarity with cloud-native secrets, identity, and access management (e.g. Key Vault, Managed Identities, RBAC).
Experience with observability tooling such as Prometheus, Grafana, Azure Monitor, or ELK/EFK.
Experience with hybrid on-premises to cloud networking (e.g. VPN, ExpressRoute).
Strong understanding of security principles and best practices for cloud environments.
Excellent communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively in a team environment.
