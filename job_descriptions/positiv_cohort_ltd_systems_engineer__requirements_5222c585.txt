We're looking for an experienced Technical Requirements Systems Engineer to drive the definition, structure, and governance of system and software requirements for a complex, high‑impact engineering programme.

This is a critical role at the heart of delivery — translating stakeholder needs into clear, testable, and traceable requirements that underpin system design, integration, and validation.

If you thrive on bringing structure to complexity and enjoy working across systems, software, and testing domains, we'd love to hear from you.

🔍 About The Role

You'll take ownership of the requirements lifecycle — from early stakeholder engagement through to detailed specification — ensuring alignment across engineering, development, and test teams.

You'll lead the development of both high‑level and detailed specifications, with a strong focus on traceability, governance, and real-world testability.

🧩 Key Responsibilities

🔹 Requirements Analysis & Governance


Analyse and refine system requirements to align with programme objectives
Translate stakeholder needs into clear, structured, and testable requirements
Develop and maintain a robust requirements governance framework
Traceability (end-to-end)
Version control
Change management

🔹 Stakeholder Engagement


Collaborate with a wide range of stakeholders, including:
End users of test systems
System designers and engineers
Software developers
Integration & test teams
Facilitate workshops, reviews, and requirement validation sessions
Ensure alignment between technical delivery and business needs


🔹 Documentation & Specification


Own the creation and progression of:
Technical Requirements Specification (TRS)
Software Requirements Specification (SRS)
Produce high-quality documentation that is:
Clear, complete, and unambiguous
Structured and maintainable
Fit for engineering and test teams


🔹 System & Software Requirements Development


Define end-to-end system requirements including:
Hardware
Software
Interfaces
Constraints
Break down high-level requirements into:
Detailed software functionality
Performance and behavioural requirements
Ensure full traceability between TRS and SRS


🔹 Integration, Test & Simulation


Ensure requirements are testable and measurable
Align requirements with system integration and validation strategies
Support the use of simulation-led testing to validate system behaviour


📦 Key Deliverables

✅ Technical Requirements Specification (TRS)

A complete, high-level requirements document covering the full test system, including:


Stakeholder-driven inputs
Hardware, software, and interface requirements
System constraints and dependencies


✅ Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

A detailed, structured breakdown of software requirements that:


Defines system functionality and behaviour
Links directly back to TRS (full traceability)
Supports development, integration, and testing


🛠️ What We're Looking For


Strong experience in requirements engineering within complex systems environments
Proven track record delivering TRS / SRS documentation
Deep understanding of:
System integration & testing
Verification & validation processes
Simulation-based testing approaches
Experience with requirements management tools (e.g. DOORS, Jama, Polarion)
Excellent stakeholder engagement and communication skills
Highly structured, detail-oriented, and analytical mindset


🎯 Why Join?


Work at the centre of a technically complex, high-impact programme
Shape how requirements drive design, development, and testing
Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams across engineering, software, and operations
Play a key role in ensuring successful system delivery
