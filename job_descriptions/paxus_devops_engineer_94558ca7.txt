DevOps Engineer | Permanent | Brisbane



Build, automate and optimise modern cloud platforms in a role where your DevOps expertise will make a real impact.

We're looking for an experienced DevOps Engineer to join a growing technology team within a complex and technology-driven organisation.

This is a full-time permanent opportunity for someone who enjoys solving complex technical problems, automating processes and helping development teams deliver software faster, more reliably and securely.



What You'll Be Doing



You'll play a key role in the ongoing evolution of the organisation's cloud and DevOps capability, working across development, infrastructure and delivery teams.

Your responsibilities will include:

Designing, building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines and automated deployment processes.
Developing and managing Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using technologies such as Terraform, Bicep or similar.
Supporting and optimising Microsoft Azure environments.
Automating infrastructure provisioning, configuration and operational processes.
Working closely with developers and technical teams to improve engineering and deployment practices.
Implementing monitoring, logging and performance improvements across cloud environments.
Troubleshooting complex infrastructure, deployment and environment issues.
Contributing to DevOps standards, governance, security and continuous improvement.
Helping drive the adoption of modern DevOps and cloud engineering practices across the organisation.


What We're Looking For



We're looking for a hands-on DevOps professional who enjoys automation, cloud technology and continuous improvement.

You'll ideally have experience across:

Microsoft Azure / Azure DevOps
CI/CD pipelines and YAML
Terraform, Bicep or other Infrastructure as Code tools
Git / GitHub
Docker and/or Kubernetes
Cloud infrastructure and automation
PowerShell or scripting
Monitoring, logging and observability
Agile delivery environments

Experience working within a large, complex or highly regulated environment will be highly regarded.



Why Join?



This is more than just a DevOps role - it's an opportunity to have genuine ownership and influence over how technology is delivered.

You'll have the opportunity to:

Join a permanent team and build long-term career opportunities.
Work with modern cloud, automation and DevOps technologies.
Contribute to meaningful technology and digital initiatives.
Help shape and improve DevOps practices and ways of working.
Work alongside experienced technology professionals in a collaborative environment.
Continue developing your technical capability across Azure, automation, IaC and CI/CD.
Enjoy the stability of a permanent position with a flexible Brisbane-based working environment.

If you're a DevOps Engineer who enjoys automation, cloud technology and making things work better, we'd love to hear from you.
