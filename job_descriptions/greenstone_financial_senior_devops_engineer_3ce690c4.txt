Be part of something transformational during an exciting period of growth within a dynamic team. The Platform Engineering team is a recently established function at Greenstone designed to drive our transformation toward Agile methodologies, modern AI-supported engineering practices, and technology modernization. As a Senior DevOps Engineer, you will enable stream-aligned teams by defining patterns and standards ("golden paths"), building automated infrastructure, and championing an exceptional developer experience.

This Will Be Achieved Through


AWS Landing Zone: Implement and validate enterprise landing zone capabilities using AWS Landing Zone Accelerator and AWS Control Tower patterns
Infrastructure as Code: Translate AWS 3.0 architecture decisions into deployable IaC modules, pipelines, configurations, and operational runbooks
Policy as Code: Implement and validate pre- and post-deployment assurance controls, exception handling, and compliance evidence
Design Governance: Constructively challenge weak designs, unclear requirements, and manual "click-ops" operating models using evidence-based reasoning
Team Enablement: Lead solution designs with support from Staff/Principal Engineers, and mentor team members through collaborative pairing and hands-on leadership by example


What are we looking for?


Strong communication and influencing skills
A practical, delivery-focused approach
Proven experience in a transformation as a Senior DevOps or Platform Engineer turning complex architecture, security, and networking requirements into working AWS patterns
Deep technical expertise in AWS enterprise landing zones, IaC module development, and automated pipelines
Experience establishing compliant, automated platforms fit for regulated workloads

Who We Are

Greenstone distributes insurance products under trusted brands in Australia, New Zealand and Canada. Some of our brands are Buddy, Australian Seniors, New Zealand Seniors, OneChoice, Real Insurance, SPCA Pet Insurance, North Cover, FiftyUp and Guardian.

We are the leading distributor of direct Insurance products in the Australian insurance market. Since launching into the Financial Services sector in 2007, Greenstone has experienced a commanding volume of growth being recognised for our credible brands, customer focused values and people-driven work culture.

We are passionate about our core values, which contribute towards the positive culture that supports our employees, our customers and our community.
