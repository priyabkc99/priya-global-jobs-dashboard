Full job description

An exciting opportunity has arisen to recruit a Quality Engineer whereby you will play a key part in supporting, controlling, and inspecting technical processes to ensure a high standard of quality throughout the organisation.

As a Quality Engineer, your responsibilities will include:

• Quality Control Plans (QCPs): Establish, apply, and maintain comprehensive quality control plans, ensuring they are implemented efficiently and accurately.

• Data Analysis: Analyse medium to complex system data to inform decisions and drive precise execution throughout the organisation.

• Project Involvement: Participate in the design and implementation phases of projects, ensuring that quality is embedded at every stage.

• Regulatory Compliance: Stay informed of the latest regulatory requirements and coach internal teams on their application and proper usage.

• Audits and Assessments: Conduct and support internal and external audits, evaluations, and assessments to ensure compliance with ISO, ASTM, DIN, BSI, and other relevant standards.

• Supplier Quality Management: Perform supplier inspections to ensure that their quality standards align with Urenco’s requirements and project expectations.




Key requirements and responsibilities of the role will include:

• Technical Background: A solid foundation in engineering (electrical), with a clear understanding of technical business processes.

• Standards Expertise: In-depth knowledge of international quality standards, including ISO, ASTM, DIN, and BSI.

• Quality Control Experience: Proven experience in establishing and maintaining quality control processes, including the development of integrated QCPs.

• Certification & Auditing: Experience with certification processes in technical industries and the ability to conduct quality audits and supplier inspections.




Inside IR35.

Please feel free to get in touch to discuss things in more detail if you match the above criteria and it is something that would be of interest to you.
